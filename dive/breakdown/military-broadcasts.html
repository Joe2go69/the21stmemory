Military Broadcasts

Overview

The EBS Operation (Emergency Broadcast System) is a highly synchronized, multi-stage intervention coordinated by the White Hats and allied military forces to systematically dismantle the Parasitic Overlay and awaken humanity. Triggered after a series of staged geopolitical crises, the military seizes complete control of the airwaves to bypass traditional Mainstream Media (MSM) and deliver undeniable Truth Packages directly to the public. This operation establishes a highly controlled environment, utilizing a massive communications blackout and physical military protection to force the population to absorb critical disclosures without inciting total societal collapse.

Key Terminology

EBS Operation

A multi-stage activation process where military and White Hat forces commandeer all media and internet channels to broadcast suppressed truths to the global population.

Truth Packages

Over 72 hours of undeniable evidence and disclosures revealing corruption, human trafficking, and the true nature of global control structures, broadcast continuously during the EBS.

White Hats

The allied human and off-world forces, including the Global Alliance Army (G.A.A.) and Space Force, responsible for executing the EBS and dismantling the parasitic control systems.

Communications Cut/Blackout

A deliberate severing of internet and traditional media lines to prevent parasitic forces from twisting the narrative prior to the EBS activation.

Military Stabilization Lockdowns

Protective measures enacted during the EBS broadcasts, placing military personnel on the streets to maintain order and allow the public to safely absorb the disclosures.

Scare Events

Staged crises, such as a simulated World War III and financial collapse, designed to stress the public into paying full attention to the emergency broadcasts.

Core Revelations

The EBS is not a mere emergency alert; it is the ultimate psychological and energetic weapon deployed to shatter the False Reality of the Sleepers and NPCs in one decisive blow. The broadcasts are meticulously staged, beginning with soft reassurance before escalating into severe, paradigm-shifting truths, ensuring the population does not entirely collapse from trauma. Furthermore, the military presence during the EBS is strictly for civilian protection and stabilization, contrasting sharply with the parasitic control lockdowns of the past.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

Pre-EBS Conditioning: Before the EBS is triggered, orchestrated scare events, such as staged missile strikes and market crashes, push the collective consciousness to the brink of panic. This ensures that when the broadcast begins, the public is completely focused, desperate for answers, and unable to ignore the incoming transmission.

The Blackout: During the first 72 hours of the operation, major internet cables are severed and traditional communications go dark. The military subsequently assumes total control over all media and broadcasting channels.

Initial Broadcasts (Soft Truths): The first messages are strictly for immediate reassurance. The military explicitly broadcasts a clear message: "STAY CALM YOU ARE SAFE, MILITARY CONTROL ACTIVE TO PROTECT YOU (NO POLICE)," establishing immediate trust, bypassing NPC panic loops, and setting the stage for the coming revelations.

The Flood Gates (Harder Truths): Once the population is stabilized, the EBS transitions into delivering hard truths. This stage releases verifiable proof of election fraud, the Federal Reserve scam, child trafficking rings, satanic cults, and the reality that global leaders were entirely controlled by parasite systems.

The 72+ Hour Window: The disclosures run continuously for over 72 hours, intentionally designed to cause the mental breakdowns necessary to fracture the 3D Simulation. This period acts as a collective "pause button," forcing the entire world to sit still, watch, process the trauma, and reset.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The EBS operation is deeply interconnected with the Staged Alien Invasion and Project Blue Beam mechanics. If the true off-world fleets uncloaked in the sky before the EBS disclosures provided the necessary context, the public would assume an alien attack and collapse in terror. Conversely, if the EBS aired without preceding scare events, the sleeping masses would remain distracted and switch it off.

The operation acts as the primary catalyst for the Frequency Fracture. As the military media begins dropping these massive truth packages, the resulting shockwaves of awakening among human and E.T. Sols destabilize the entire parasitic energy grid. The military lockdowns accompanying the EBS provide the exact physical environment needed to process the incoming Light Grids and frequencies without the chaotic interference of surging crowds.

Strategic Implications

The staged execution of the EBS ensures that the parasitic narrative is permanently dismantled while keeping the public insulated from hostile AI programming and fear loops. By secretly replacing compromised global leaders with Clones, Stand-in Actors, and holograms long before the event, the White Hats bought the critical time needed to position military assets for the EBS without prematurely triggering societal collapse.

Ultimately, the EBS prepares humanity for the Mass Reveal Window and the subsequent Sky Opening. It serves as the bridge that transitions the population from a state of controlled blindness to the realization of their true existence within the Great Dome, paving the way for the true fleets to arrive and the extraction of resonating souls.
