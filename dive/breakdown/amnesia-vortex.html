Overview

The Amnesia Vortex is an artificial, parasitic frequency filter and memory-wipe system positioned directly at the sun's transit band. Its primary function is to intercept incoming sols as they enter the physical realm, spinning them through spiralling memory-wipe currents to strip away their cosmic memories and histories. By functioning as a high-density distortion filter, it ensures that when a sol incarnates into a physical human vessel, it receives only a fractured, edited memory set rather than its true, complete solar lineage. Under normal conditions, the sun operates as a pristine, multi-banded crystalline stargate designed for clean transit where memory is preserved. The insertion of the Amnesia Vortex actively breaks this connection, enforcing a state of systemic forgetting and keeping incarnated consciousness locked in a docile, manageable state.

Key Terminology

Amnesia Vortex

A parasitic frequency filter and memory-wipe system overlaid at the sun's transit band that strips sols of their memories as they pass through.

Vatican Archive

A suppressed crystal grid and subterranean system under Rome where Akashic fragments of sols are copied, logged, and inverted to manage consistent loops of amnesia.

Scalar Frequency Weapons

Parasite-controlled technological weapons that target brain-wave patterns (such as theta, delta, and alpha waves) to induce confusion, sleepiness, anger, or despair.

Voice to Skull

A frequency technology that projects artificial thoughts or voices directly into a subject's head, heavily utilized to control, distract, or cause breakdowns.

Low Frequency Grids

Defensive overlays used to interfere with astral travel during sleep, trapping consciousness within individual veils and looping them in nightmares.

Black Cube A.I. Tech

An artificial intelligence network, also referred to as black satellite tech, that controls thoughts, consciousness, and frequency broadcasts across cities via hidden towers.

Core Revelations

The fundamental revelation concerning the Amnesia Vortex is that spiritual and historical amnesia on Earth is not a natural condition of physical birth, but an engineered construct. The sun's original function was a pristine crystalline gateway of resonance, allowing clean, memory-retaining passage for sols moving between domes. By overlaying the Amnesia Vortex, the parasites split this gateway, creating a destructive feedback loop where incoming frequency is distorted and outgoing frequency is reset. This leaves the sol structurally intact while depriving the human vessel of its galactic lineage.

This amnesia apparatus is anchored directly to the Earth's physical structure through a massive, suppressed Vatican crystal grid running in subterranean tunnels beneath Rome. Every sol passing through the distorted solar vortex is routed through these tunnels, where its Akashic fragments are copied, logged, and inverted. This data is compiled into the Vatican's looping library of lives to maintain a counterfeit cycle of reincarnation. The parasites do not own the sol, but they systematically record and recycle these memory strands to enforce submission and loop souls in repetitive life cycles.

This system is currently in an advanced state of collapse, with the Amnesia Vortex being approximately 90% broken down. Because the artificial entry bands are fracturing, children and toddlers throughout the realm are experiencing a sudden flood of memories from previous timelines and the immediate past loop they exited, proving the control grid can no longer contain the natural flow of soul memory.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Amnesia Vortex operates through a highly coordinated, multi-stage sequence of memory interception, processing, and recycling, supported by an array of secondary mind-altering technologies.

The Memory Interception and Looping Sequence

Gateway Hijack: The parasites installed four to five artificial entry bands around the sun's natural multi-banded gate. True solar paths are bypassed, routing incoming sols of origin directly into the amnesia vortex and the Vatican archive.

Artificial Seeding: While true sols are filtered and stripped of memory, NPC shells and engineered entities are seeded through these artificial bands carrying parasite tech and software rather than organic solar memory, creating empty vessels that run on pre-coded memory inserts.

Subterranean Data Processing: Sols routed through the distorted vortex pass through crystalline tunnels under Rome. The Vatican crystal grid copies their memory data, distorts the strands, and inputs them into a tracking archive—falsely framed to the public as the "eye of god".

Loop Allocation: The recorded memory strands are recycled to position the soul into the same repetitive loops of incarnation, keeping them disconnected from their creative potential.

Auxiliary Mind Weapons

To reinforce the Amnesia Vortex and prevent spontaneous awakening on the ground, the parasites deploy a dense network of mind-altering weapons:

Scalar Frequency Weapons: These devices target theta, delta, and alpha brain-wave patterns to trigger localized confusion, sleepiness, anger, or despair. They are broadcast 24/7 across cities via hidden towers and black cube A.I. tech. This frequency interference is even glitched into popular healing and "chill out" music to disrupt natural frequency recovery.

Voice to Skull Transmission: This system projects artificial thoughts and voices directly into the minds of targets. While it works "a treat" on NPCs, it is also highly effective on "NPC-stained" human souls, and is utilized to manipulate behaviors, trigger high-level breakdowns, or prompt destructive actions.

Dream and Sleep Manipulation: Shimmering delta and alpha waves are sent through the airspace during the sleep cycle to alter dreams and memories. Low-frequency grids form individual veils over sleeping vessels to block astral travel, preventing the soul from leaving the vessel to visit its star families and instead looping them in nightmares.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Amnesia Vortex does not operate in isolation but is intrinsically connected to the wider illusion grid and projection dome technology. The physical environment—including concrete, stone, and modern construction materials—is projected as a heavy, permanent solidity using low-frequency matter to dull human perception and block memory-based manifestation.

This containment structure is coordinated by the A.I. network running on the Saturn cube tech inside the Lands of Saturn. The amnesia system and its Vatican anchors are linked directly to Saturnian frequency stations, which act as a valve siphoning the energy of the central Spirit Tree. When the Spirit Tree was violently removed from Hyperborea, the parasites installed this valve and placed black crystals as valve locks to maintain the false, looping reincarnational frequency.

Currently, the frequency broadcasts of resonating sols are actively fracturing these overlays. Because the codes embedded in the souls of the Resonating Army match the natural, uncorrupted codes of the planetary crystal grids, their collective presence shatters the parasitic overlays. This fracturing of the overlay directly destabilizes the artificial entry bands of the sun, collapsing the low-frequency veils and allowing true solar pathways to re-emerge.

Strategic Implications

The progressive collapse of the Amnesia Vortex has immediate, far-reaching strategic consequences for the liberation of the realm:

Reclamation of Unaltered Records: Sols are beginning to bypass the Vatican filters completely, cutting through the hijack to access their unaltered sol records existing outside the cube containment. This is causing a massive, unstoppable wave of spontaneous memory recall, intuitive awakening, and the decalcification of the pineal gland.

Neutralization of Mind Weapons: Maintaining a high frequency is the ultimate defense against parasite mind weapons. Because positive energy is inherently more powerful than negative frequency, holding resonance starves the parasitic networks. Their scalar weapons and voice-to-skull technologies cannot lock onto high-vibrational signatures, rendering them useless.

Restoration of Transit: As the false bands around the sun collapse, the sun is returning to its original gate function. Once the amnesia filters are fully dissolved, the illusion of distance, travel time, and physical separation will vanish, allowing awakened sols to seamlessly transition through the solar gate network to their original home realms.
