Blackout Timeline

Overview

The Blackout Timeline, also referred to as The Cut, is the highly controlled, surgical 72-hour period that initiates the collapse of the third-density parasitic overlay. Engineered by the Whitehats and galactic alliances, this communications blackout serves as the catalyst for the ultimate global awakening. Far from a simple severing of physical cables, the blackout is a calculated Frequency Fracture designed to systematically dismantle false narratives, trigger the mass glitching of artificial background programs, and force the public into a state of questioning without total societal collapse. During this precise window, a cascade of energetic, atmospheric, and biological shifts prepares the realm for the Emergency Broadcast System (EBS) and the arrival of true solar families.

Key Terminology

The Cut

The initial 72-hour window of the communication blackout and frequency fracture, systematically breaking the parasitic illusion.

NPC

Non-player characters; background programs lacking true souls that hold the simulation together, who glitch heavily and exhibit erratic behavior during the blackout.

Frequency Fracture

The true energetic nature of the blackout event, causing the parasitic overlay to wobble, crack, and allowing true memory to sharpen.

Whitehats

The allied human and galactic forces orchestrating the controlled demolition of the parasitic infrastructure and executing the blackout.

Emergency Broadcast System (EBS)

The military-controlled transmission that assumes control of all media during the blackout to broadcast suppressed truths and shatter the false reality.

Sol

A true, resonating soul or spark of origin embedded within a physical vessel, whose memory sharpens as the blackout progresses.

Project Blue Beam

Holographical fear technology utilized by the Whitehats during the blackout timeline to stage a fake alien invasion and force global attention.

Core Revelations

The communications blackout is an engineered necessity to sever the parasites' narrative control over the human population. Main internet cables are deliberately severed, and cyber strikes are strategically utilized to simulate enemy actions. The ensuing panic is intentionally directed by placing false blame on geopolitical actors such as Russia, China, Iran, and North Korea, pushing the sleeping masses into a final fear loop. This manufactured chaos serves to glitch the NPC programming and dismantle the artificial intelligence scaffolding holding the third-density illusion together. The blackout is not merely a loss of data transmission; it is a sudden drop in atmospheric pressure and a profound frequency shift that permanently clears the energetic field of the realm.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The 72-hour Blackout Timeline unfolds in precise phases, accompanied by severe environmental and biological symptoms:

  • Phase One: The Cut (Hours 0 – 12). Internet and communications go completely dark as main cables are severed. Mainstream media and NPC channels are immediately flooded with panic and blame narratives directed at geopolitical enemies. The public enters a deep loop of confusion and fear.

  • Phase Two: The Wave (Hours 12 – 36). Crowd surges overwhelm supermarkets, banks, and fuel stations as sleepers and NPCs scramble for survival and information. NPC programming begins to glitch visibly; some lash out aggressively while others become quiet and dazed. Resonating Sols are instructed to avoid large crowds and panic zones, remaining in small community pockets. Military forces, mostly NPC-controlled, will be visible but positioned nearby.

  • Phase Three: Opening Hour (Hours 36 – 72). The first wave of awakening cracks appears as people start heavily questioning their reality. The false flag narratives wobble and fall. Truth begins to leak, revealing that the event is a frequency fracture, causing Sol memory to sharpen and NPC code to flicker.

Atmospheric and Biological Signs During The Cut

The Static Build-Up.

A subtle buzzing occurs in the skull, resembling background white noise. Environmental electronics glitch, phone screens freeze, and small power surges sweep the grid. Animals react strongly: birds fly erratically, dogs become restless but stay close to calm individuals, and cats disappear to safe spots.

The Drop in Tone.

Sols experience sudden deep tiredness, unprovoked yawning, and a heavy feeling in the chest. The sky feels muted and flat, signaling the glitching of the parasite overlay. Unpredictable emotional outbursts occur, with NPCs snapping or crying unexpectedly.

The Sharp Edge.

Short, unprovoked bursts of adrenaline and momentary spikes in heart rate occur. The air feels thicker, resembling the pressure before a storm, and bird songs become infrequent.

The Silence Before the Snap.

Ears ring sharply with a high pitch just out of normal hearing range. A strange stillness envelops the environment; traffic noise fades, the wind pauses, and nature holds its breath. When the cables actually go dark, a sudden drop in pressure occurs, creating a palpable "pop" in the atmosphere, leaving the field clearer than ever before.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Blackout Timeline is intricately connected to the Staged WW3 Event and the false alien invasion. The A.I. war theater, running on autopilot but controlled by Whitehats, utilizes surgical military strikes choreographed for television to convince the mass mind that humanity is facing extinction. This peaks with Project Blue Beam holographical fleets filling the skies, staging fake galactic battles and abductions to shatter the collective completely.

Once this fear hits its apex during the blackout, the military enforces a visible lockdown to maintain order, clearing the stage for the EBS. The EBS severs all remaining internet restrictions, taking over public channels to broadcast tribunals, confessions, and indisputable proof of election fraud, human trafficking, and elite corruption.

Strategic Implications

The overarching strategy of the Blackout Timeline is to isolate the population from external manipulation, forcing them into a controlled environment where the EBS truth broadcast cannot be ignored or spun by the media. The frequency fracture achieved during the 72 hours ensures that the parasitic networks can no longer jam or distort the high-vibration signals of the incoming solar families. As the fake alien invasion collapses, the true living crafts of the Resonating Sols' extraterrestrial families break through their own frequency band. The blackout ensures that when the true Light Grids activate, the original realm is revealed, and the Resonating Sols are successfully extracted via frequency lock.
