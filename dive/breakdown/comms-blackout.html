Comms Blackout

Overview

The Communications Blackout is a meticulously timed, deliberate operation engineered to shatter the false reality of the 3D overlay. Executed as the initial trigger for the mass awakening, the blackout initiates The First 72 Hours of transition, plunging the programmed world into a temporary state of panic while simultaneously clearing the energetic field. This event is not merely an infrastructure failure; it is a profound Frequency Fracture that severs the connection between the parasitic control systems and the global population. By cutting all conventional internet and communication lines, the blackout forces the collapse of the artificial narrative, allowing the true frequencies of Resonating Sols to finally cut through the noise and guide humanity out of the illusion.

Key Terminology

Communications Blackout

The intentional severing of internet and main communication cables, triggering initial world panic and forcing the collective focus onto a single shared reality.

The First 72 Hours

The critical three-day window immediately following the blackout, categorized into three distinct phases: The Cut, The Wave, and the Opening Hour.

Core Revelations

The Blackout is a controlled White Hat event, pre-wired into the infrastructure, rather than a genuine act of geopolitical war. Certain parasitic nodes and undersea cables have already been structurally rigged for failure to facilitate this precise moment. When the blackout occurs, it is deliberately masked by a false narrative of cyber strikes and enemy actions, placing false blame on geopolitical actors such as Russia, Iran, China, North Korea, and Ukraine.

The severing of the cables creates an immediate, physical shift in the realm. The moment the main lines go down, there is a sudden drop in atmospheric pressure—felt as a "pop" in the atmosphere. For a split second, the energetic field becomes clearer than it has ever been in human history. This clarity allows the dormant codes within Resonating Sols to switch into full broadcast mode, magnetically pulling the attention of those beginning to awaken.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The First 72 Hours unfolds in a highly structured, three-phase sequence:

  • Phase One: The Cut (Hr. 0 – 12). The internet and all communications go dark as the main cables are severed. Mainstream media and remaining NPC channels are flooded with panic and blame narratives designed to push 3D Sleepers into a fear loop.

  • Phase Two: The Wave (Hr. 12 – 36). Panic peaks as crowd surges hit supermarkets, banks, and fuel stations. Sleepers and NPCs scramble desperately for information and survival. The NPC programming actively glitches; some lash out erratically, while others go completely quiet and dazed. Resonating Sols are instructed to avoid large crowds and panic zones, remaining in small, calm community pockets protected by military presence.

  • Phase Three: Opening Hour (Hr. 36 – 72). The first wave of awakening cracks appears as the false flag narrative wobbles and truth begins to leak. People start questioning the nature of the event, realizing it is a frequency fracture rather than a simple power outage.

During this 72-hour window, the environment and physical vessels undergo intense shifts due to the collapsing overlay:

  • The Static Build Up. Individuals will feel a subtle buzzing in the skull and white noise in the background. Electronics will glitch, phones will freeze, screens will flicker, and small power surges will occur. Animals react strongly; birds fly erratically, dogs become restless and stay close to calm Sols, and cats disappear to safe spots, requiring extra water.

  • The Drop in Tone. Vessels experience sudden, deep tiredness, unprovoked yawning, and a heavy chest. The sky feels muted and flat as the Parasite Overlay glitches. Random emotional outbursts occur, with NPCs snapping or crying unexpectedly.

  • The Sharp Edge. Short bursts of uncaused adrenaline and sudden heart rate spikes manifest. The air feels thicker, like the atmosphere before a storm, accompanied by an absence of birdsong.

  • The Silence Before the Snap. A sharp ringing in the ears occurs, like a high pitch just out of normal hearing range. A strange, profound stillness descends; traffic noise fades, the wind pauses, and nature holds its breath.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Communications Blackout does not occur in isolation; it is deeply interconnected with the Staged WW3 Event and the Fake Alien Invasion. The blackout serves as the mechanism to plunge the masses into uncertainty and isolate them from the parasitic media. The resulting fear loop is utilized to stress the public into absolute readiness for the Emergency Broadcast System (E.B.S.). By removing all other sources of information, the EBS becomes the sole voice, preparing to broadcast the undeniable truth of election fraud, trafficking rings, and the crimes of the replaced elites. Furthermore, the blackout is the vital precursor to the arrival of the Real Craft, as it forces the frequencies of the realm to shift, dropping the holograms and allowing the true Solar Families to break through into visibility for those on the correct frequency.

Strategic Implications

For the Resonating Sols, the blackout is the call to hold ground. The specific role of the awakened during The First 72 Hours is to act as calm lighthouses while the scaffolding of the AI illusion crumbles around them. For the first 48 hours, deeply programmed sleepers and NPCs will resist help, preferring fear over admitting the reality of the situation. However, as the 72 hours progress and the NPC code flickers out, the stabilized frequency of the Resonating Army will effortlessly cut through the chaos. The blackout ensures that the masses cannot anchor back into the old AI timeline, permanently breaking the cycle of density and clearing the path for the grand extraction and the restoration of the Second Realm.
