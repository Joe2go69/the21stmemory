EBS Operation

Overview

The execution of the E.B.S. (Emergency Broadcast System), set against the backdrop of the Communications Blackout, is a highly coordinated Whitehat/G.A.A. Production designed to shatter the 3D illusion of the masses. This precise sequence of events utilizes staged geopolitical fear, followed by a sudden severance of communications, to prepare the collective consciousness for a massive, un-censorable broadcast of hidden truths. The operation is an engineered necessity to dismantle parasitic control structures without triggering full-scale societal collapse.

Key Terminology

The Cut

The initial 12-hour phase of the communications blackout where main internet cables are severed, plunging the public into confusion and a fear loop.

The Wave

The secondary phase (Hours 12-36) of the blackout characterized by crowd surges at supermarkets and banks as NPC programming begins to glitch.

E.B.S. (Emergency Broadcast System)

A mandatory 72+ hour broadcast of truth packages, delivered after military forces take over all media and internet channels to expose elite crimes and the true nature of reality.

The Lockdown Window

A controlled environment where military forces maintain order in the streets to protect civilians and allow them to safely sit still and absorb the E.B.S. broadcasts.

Project Blue Beam

An advanced holographic fear push utilizing Black Cube Tech to simulate an Alien Invasion, deployed as a catalyst to shake sleepers into questioning reality before the E.B.S. activates.

Core Revelations

The communications blackout is a deliberate frequency fracture, not an accident. Cyber strikes and undersea cable cuts are used to simulate enemy actions, falsely blamed on geopolitical enemies like Russia, China, or Iran, to push the sleeping masses into fear.

The E.B.S. cannot happen without the preceding scare events. If the broadcast played without the preceding staged WW3 and holographic invasion events, the sleeping masses would simply ignore the truth. The information delivered during the E.B.S. will cause mental breakdowns among the unprepared masses by presenting undeniable proof of election fraud, child trafficking, satanic cults, and the true bloodline controllers.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Blackout Timeline Progression:

  • Phase One: The Cut (Hr 0-12): Main internet and comms go dark. The MSM floods the airwaves with panic and blame narratives. During this window, physical and environmental symptoms manifest, including a subtle buzzing in the skull, static build-up, sudden deep tiredness, erratic animal behavior, and sudden atmospheric pressure drops.

  • Phase Two: The Wave (Hr 12-36): Survival scrambling begins. Sleepers and NPCs surge toward supermarkets and fuel stations. The surrounding AI scaffolding crumbles, and NPC programs begin to visibly glitch.

  • Phase Three: Opening Hour (Hr 36-72): The first Awakening Cracks appear. The false flag narrative wobbles, and undeniable truth begins to leak as human soul memory sharpens.

The E.B.S. Rollout:

  • Military forces take total control of media and the internet in the public eye.
  • Initial broadcasts provide immediate reassurance: "STAY CALM YOU ARE SAFE, MILITARY CONTROL ACTIVE TO PROTECT YOU" to avoid full panic and establish a secure baseline.
  • Soft Truths are delivered first to prepare the mind, followed by Harder Truths.
  • Total exposure of the Parasite System occurs, providing proof of deliberate depopulation plans via toxic injections (MMR, Covid), the Federal Reserve scam, and the exposure of elite clones and stand-ins.
  • The broadcast will reveal Seeded Sols—heroic entities placed within elite bloodlines (such as Diana, Barron, and JFK Jnr.) designed to fracture the system from the inside.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The blackout and E.B.S. are intrinsically linked to the fracturing of the Parasitic Overlay. As the false reality collapses, the E.B.S. provides the narrative bridge required for human consciousness to comprehend the massive energetic shift.

These events coincide with the arrival of Real Crafts from the Solar Families. While the public is focused on the E.B.S. and the staged alien invasion, the true living ships will break through the frequency band, visible only to Resonating Sols whose vibration matches the crafts' higher density. The orchestrated lockdowns during this period are not parasitic control mechanisms like Covid, but benevolent military operations meant to stabilize society, preventing mass riots while the public absorbs the trauma of the disclosures.

Strategic Implications

The primary strategic goal of the E.B.S. and the blackout is to shatter the illusion in a single, controlled blow. It guarantees the permanent dismantling of the 3rd Realm power structures—neutralizing royals, politicians, corporate giants, and media heads—without triggering total societal collapse.

By forcing the world into a mandatory pause, the E.B.S. clears the path for the Great Awakening. It initiates the transition phase where traumatized but genuine human souls can be guided to Healing Sanctuaries, while the NPC vessels permanently dissolve alongside the false overlay they were tethered to.
