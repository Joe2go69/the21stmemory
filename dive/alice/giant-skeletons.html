Overview

The current historical narrative is a manufactured illusion designed to conceal the cyclical destruction of advanced human civilizations through a mechanical, catastrophic process known as a Re-set. Following these planet-wide events, remnants of the preceding world remain embedded in the landscape, directly contradicting the timeline enforced by modern institutions. The most profound and aggressively suppressed physical evidence of these prior epochs are the remains of Giants, which were discovered en masse during periods of rapid post-reset infrastructural expansion. These immense skeletal remains, alongside vast architectural ruins, petrified anomalies, and high-frequency technological relics, serve as undeniable proof of a hidden timeline that shatters the fabricated doctrine of Evolution. To maintain the illusion of linear human progress from primitive origins, an exhaustive, multi-generational cover-up was orchestrated by shadow entities and secret societies to systematically confiscate and obscure this evidence from the global population.

Key Terminology

Re-set

A globally orchestrated, catastrophic termination and harvesting of a civilization, designed to wipe out accumulated knowledge, infrastructure, and adult populations, followed by the repopulation of the realm with memory-wiped clones and orphans.

Oopa&#39;s (Out Of Place Artefacts)

Blatant physical evidence and embarrassing technological or biological remnants from highly advanced civilizations that existed prior to a recent reset, deliberately hidden from public view.

Giants

Highly varied, exceptionally large humanoid biological entities whose skeletal remains measure anywhere from 12 feet to over 35 meters in height, entombed in mounds across the terrain.

Smithsonian Institute

An institution functioning as the "Vatican of Archaeology," founded and controlled by secret societies to aggressively confiscate, hide, and hoard physical anomalies like giant skeletons.

Freemasons

A deeply embedded secret society functioning at the behest of parasitic overlords, responsible for rewriting history, executing resets, suppressing old-world technology, and monopolizing public knowledge.

Railroad Magnates

High-ranking 33rd-degree Freemasons who controlled the post-reset expansion of coal and steam infrastructure, directly overseeing the unearthing and subsequent cover-up of giant burial mounds.

NDA (Non Disclosure Agreement)

A coercive legal instrument heavily weaponized by authorities to silence civilians who accidentally discover anomalous remains, enforced through severe threats to life, property, and freedom.

Mudflood

The physical result of advanced extraterrestrial energy weapon technology used to trigger mass soil liquefaction, intentionally burying the lower levels of grand old-world architecture during a reset.

Tartaria / Great Tartary

A globally unified, highly advanced civilization of aesthetic and technological excellence that was abruptly terminated by a reset and falsely labeled as the "Dark Ages".

Core Revelations

Scale and Dimensions of Giant Discoveries

Over 10,000 Giant remains have been systematically recovered from American soil alone. These remains exhibit staggering proportions; excavations have yielded complete skeletons ranging from 12 feet tall to an astonishing 35+ meters. Specific, isolated discoveries include massive human skulls measuring 2 meters tall by 1.5 meters wide, as well as distinct human femurs reaching up to 30 meters in length.

The Railroad Expansion Catalyst

The post-reset development of the railroads required cutting through vast, unexplored tracts of American land. Many large hills, which were completely unknown to the repopulated masses as ancient burial mounds, were sliced in half to lay train tracks. These industrial incisions exposed multiple giants of varying sizes entombed within the same earthen structures.

The Orchestrated Suppression

The Railroad Magnates who ordered the land-cutting were uniformly 33rd-degree Freemasons working in lockstep with the Smithsonian Institute. Every unearthing of a giant skeleton triggered immediate confiscation by the Smithsonian, ensuring the physical evidence vanished into deep storage before public awareness could take root.

Documentary Evidence

Despite the massive scale of the cover-up, hundreds of early United States newspaper microfiche slides, stretching well into the 1970s, contain explicit reports of these giant discoveries before they were aggressively scrubbed from mainstream discourse.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Civilian Confiscation Protocol

When ordinary citizens accidentally unearth giant remains—such as a builder discovering a massive skull beneath a residential garden—they invariably contact local police out of confusion. The police escalate the anomaly to local universities, which immediately notify the Smithsonian or its international counterparts in London. Within hours, official recovery teams arrive, force the discoverer to surrender all photographic evidence, and compel them to sign a strict NDA. Discoverers are subjected to severe threats of long-term imprisonment, loss of property, and harm to their families if they ever break their silence.

The Vaults of Concealment

Once confiscated, giant skeletons and Oopa's are transported to sealed vaults beneath the Smithsonian. Much like the 13 hidden subterranean levels beneath the Vatican, these sub-basement vaults are strictly off-limits to the public and accessible only to elite bloodline members and the parasitic controllers themselves.

Energy Weaponry and Liquefaction

Resets employ advanced extraterrestrial energy weapons that alter molecular and subatomic cohesion. This technology causes "Mudflood" soil liquefaction, burying grand infrastructure. Furthermore, these directed frequencies can instantly fuse incompatible materials or turn biological entities—including Giants, humans, trees, and everyday objects like hats and books—into petrified stone.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Fake Evolutionary Timeline

The existence of Giants and highly advanced Oopa's entirely demolishes the fabricated theory of natural evolution. The controllers, utterly unable to explain how 35-meter skeletons or advanced fossilized texts fit into a narrative of apes evolving into modern humans on a spinning globe, simply sweep the evidence under the rug to prevent public awakening.

The Grand Canyon Cover-Up

Evidence of pre-reset occupations includes distinctly Egyptian and Anuk (Anunnaki) names scattered across landmarks in the Grand Canyon, representing the Zep Tepi ("First Time") headquarters. The persistent early 20th-century reports of an underground city in this region were aggressively declared a hoax by the Smithsonian to suppress knowledge of non-human, high-technology history.

Post-Reset Repopulation and Orphans

Following the catastrophic termination of Great Tartary, surviving grand architecture was claimed by newly introduced populations. These groups were heavily composed of mind-wiped NPC (Non-Player Character) entities and cloned Orphans, grown in underground facilities, who were subsequently trained to accept a completely false historical timeline.

Lunatic Asylums as Reset Artifacts

The immediate aftermath of a reset required massive containment for the traumatized survivors who physically witnessed the global destruction. Enormous structures, quickly repurposed as Lunatic Asylums, were pre-planned into the architecture to house these catatonic victims, simultaneously functioning as massive "Loosh" energetic harvesting batteries for demonic entities.

Culling the Memory Keepers via Warfare

To ensure the cover-up of the previous civilization remained absolute, orchestrated global conflicts such as World War 1 were initiated specifically to cull the remaining population aged 15 to 55. These individuals still held generational memory of the pre-reset world, natural medicine, and the true shape of the earth, posing a direct threat to the newly imposed ignorance.

Acknowledging the reality of Giant Skeletons and previous advanced civilizations shatters the "Perceived Knowledge" string of control that binds humanity. If humanity understood that history is merely a cycle of violent resets, the entire psychological and societal control grid would collapse.

The failure to completely erase these Oopa's acts as a massive catalyst for those observing the inconsistencies, forcing individuals to deconstruct their fabricated reality and recognize the simulation they inhabit.
