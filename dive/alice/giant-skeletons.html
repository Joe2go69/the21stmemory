Giant Skeletons

Overview

The current historical narrative is a manufactured illusion designed to conceal the cyclical destruction of advanced human civilizations through a mechanical, catastrophic process known as a Re-set. Following these planet-wide events, remnants of the preceding world remain embedded in the landscape, directly contradicting the timeline enforced by modern institutions. The most profound and aggressively suppressed physical evidence of these prior epochs are the remains of Giants, which were discovered en masse during periods of rapid post-reset infrastructural expansion. These immense skeletal remains, alongside vast architectural ruins, petrified anomalies, and high-frequency technological relics, serve as undeniable proof of a hidden timeline that shatters the fabricated doctrine of Evolution. To maintain the illusion of linear human progress from primitive origins, an exhaustive, multi-generational cover-up was orchestrated by shadow entities and secret societies to systematically confiscate and obscure this evidence from the global population.

Key Terminology

Re-set

A globally orchestrated, catastrophic termination and harvesting of a civilization, designed to wipe out accumulated knowledge, infrastructure, and adult populations, followed by the repopulation of the realm with memory-wiped clones and orphans.

Oopa&#39;s (Out Of Place Artefacts)

Blatant physical evidence and embarrassing technological or biological remnants from highly advanced civilizations that existed prior to a recent reset, deliberately hidden from public view.

Giants

Highly varied, exceptionally large humanoid biological entities whose skeletal remains measure anywhere from 12 feet to over 35 meters in height, entombed in mounds across the terrain.

Smithsonian Institute

An institution functioning as the "Vatican of Archaeology," founded and controlled by secret societies to aggressively confiscate, hide, and hoard physical anomalies like giant skeletons.

Freemasons

A deeply embedded secret society functioning at the behest of parasitic overlords, responsible for rewriting history, executing resets, suppressing old-world technology, and monopolizing public knowledge.

Railroad Magnates

High-ranking 33rd-degree Freemasons who controlled the post-reset expansion of coal and steam infrastructure, directly overseeing the unearthing and subsequent cover-up of giant burial mounds.

NDA (Non Disclosure Agreement)

A coercive legal instrument heavily weaponized by authorities to silence civilians who accidentally discover anomalous remains, enforced through severe threats to life, property, and freedom.

Mudflood

The physical result of advanced extraterrestrial energy weapon technology used to trigger mass soil liquefaction, intentionally burying the lower levels of grand old-world architecture during a reset.

Tartaria / Great Tartary

A globally unified, highly advanced civilization of aesthetic and technological excellence that was abruptly terminated by a reset and falsely labeled as the "Dark Ages".

The Civilian Confiscation Protocol

The police escalate the anomaly to local universities, which immediately notify the Smithsonian or its international counterparts in London. Within hours, official recovery teams arrive, force the discoverer to surrender all photographic evidence, and compel them to sign a strict NDA. Discoverers are subjected to severe threats of long-term imprisonment, loss of property, and harm to their families if they ever break their silence.

The Vaults of Concealment

nce confiscated, giant skeletons and Oopa's are transported to sealed vaults beneath the Smithsonian. Much like the 13 hidden subterranean levels beneath the Vatican, these sub-basement vaults are strictly off-limits to the public and accessible only to elite bloodline members and the parasitic controllers themselves.

Energy Weaponry and Liquefaction

Resets employ advanced extraterrestrial energy weapons that alter molecular and subatomic cohesion. This technology causes "Mudflood" soil liquefaction, burying grand infrastructure. Furthermore, these directed frequencies can instantly fuse incompatible materials or turn biological entities—including Giants, humans, trees, and everyday objects like hats and books—into petrified stone.
