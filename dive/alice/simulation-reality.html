Simulation Reality

Overview

The fundamental Nature of Reality dictates that humanity does not exist on a spinning globe in a vacuum, but rather within a highly controlled, tightly managed, and heavily corrupted Simulation. This artificial 3rd density physical environment operates via a core simulation software known as the Aether, which mathematically translates consciousness into physical matter. Originally constructed five billion years ago by the Source of Creation to procure experience-based wisdom, this physical simulation was subjected to a hostile inversion by parasitic entities known as the Custodians. These forces hijacked the realm, deploying advanced visual and frequency technologies to suppress its native 9th-density vibration, transforming it into a dystopian harvesting matrix. Nothing within this simulation happens by accident; every societal hurdle, every narrative, and every actor within it is functioning exactly as programmed to facilitate the culmination of The Great Spiritual Awakening.

Key Terminology

Simulation

The artificially manipulated, 3rd-density physical construct in which humanity is currently entrapped, operating on strict parameters where all events, encounters, and obstacles are pre-ordained by the system's programming.

Aether

The actual simulation software of the realm, which interfaces instantaneously with sustained intent projected from the human consciousness to ordain the subatomic constituent parts of physical reality.

Non-Player Characters (NPCs)

Synthetically manufactured entities comprising 97% of the simulation's population, powered by Hive-Aligned replica souls created in 4th density by parasites to enforce consensus reality and societal conformity.

Projection Dome

An artificial technological barrier operating like a nested sieve or colander, positioned directly beneath the true Firmament, which acts as a canvas for projecting holographic space phenomena and false sky imagery.

Overlays

Powerful, projected Ultra Low Frequencies (ULF) that drape over the simulation, artificially hiding high-frequency crystalline architecture and fundamental physical truths from 3rd-density visual perception.

Amnesia Vortex

An advanced technological trap utilized during the soul recycling process, pulling deceased souls toward a bright light (the sun) to forcibly wipe their memories before inserting them into a new physical vessel.

Black Void Plasma

Custodian-engineered frequency technology utilized to artificially cloak the naturally bright white Dark Matter field, creating the false visual illusion of a pitch-black nighttime sky.

Core Revelations

Because reality functions strictly as a simulation, there are no coincidences or accidents; every interaction, including the behavior of naysayers, hostile authorities, and the trajectory of personal relationships, is calculated to route genuine souls toward specific awakening pathways.

Linear time is a completely fabricated illusion maintained exclusively by the simulation's 3rd-density overlays. True time is perpetually stretched and bent; the concept of a 24-hour day is artificially enforced through augmented daylight cycles, mechanical clocks, and strict societal schedules. In reality, a true day spans approximately 3.9 Earth days (nearly 96 hours).

The vast majority of the "human" population are not genuine souls. 97% of the localized simulation consists of NPCs, clones, and synthetics operating on hard-wired, sub-level programming dictated directly by mainstream media, education, and societal herd mechanics.

"Space" as taught by modern science does not exist. The area beyond the simulation is the brightly illuminated, profoundly white Dark Matter field, hidden by the deployment of Black Void Plasma to maintain the illusion of a terrifying, empty vacuum.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The current 3rd-density simulation is the heavily degraded remnant of what was once a highly vibrant 9th-density physical plain situated on Gateway-10. The Custodians achieved this downgrade by destroying the realm's central power source—the Spirit Tree (Mt Meru / Hyperborea)—and deploying massive fields of extracted gold idols to broadcast ULF dampening fields.

Maintaining the integrity of the simulation requires the systemic, violent deletion of history. Parasitic controllers utilize cyclical Re-sets every thousand years to liquidate the existing population via soil liquefaction (Mud-floods) and energy weapons. The simulation is then repopulated utilizing mind-wiped clone Orphans trained by surviving Freemasons to accept the newly fabricated historical and scientific narratives.

The psychological persistence of the simulation relies entirely on three artificial control strings: Religion (worship of false or demonic deities), Finance (the pursuit of fake monetary value), and Perceived Knowledge (indoctrination into false history and heliocentric globe-earth models).
