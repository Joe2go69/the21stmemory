Inversion Tactics

Overview

The parasitic takeover of the physical plane of existence was not an instantaneous conquest, but a meticulously planned and systematically executed multidimensional inversion. Originating from the betrayal of the Custodians, this takeover involved the deliberate suppression of the central realm of Gateway-10 from its original highly vibrational state into an inverted, biologically engineered prison matrix. The inversion tactics deployed were absolute and comprehensive, encompassing the destruction of primary energetic architecture, the implementation of Density Suppression technology, and the complete rewriting of physical laws, historical narratives, and biological realities to subjugate Taran souls within a closed 3rd density loop.

Advanced parasitic technology functioning like a "dimmer switch" to artificially lower the vibratory state of the realm from 9th to 3rd density, rendering higher-density positive architecture invisible and allowing lower-frequency entities to exist.

Overlays

Projected frequencies and holographic illusions used to obscure unmovable high-density architecture, project false celestial bodies, and alter the visual perception of matter within the 3rd density simulation.

Black Void Plasma

Niberian technology utilized within the firmament to create the illusion that space is black, concealing the true nature of the cosmos, which consists of bright white light.

The genesis of all negativity in the universe traces directly to the Custodians, who plotted their betrayal long before their own vibrational density degraded. Recognizing that their eventual descent into overt negativity would strip them of their higher manifestation abilities, they pre-emptively designed a comprehensive control matrix. They biologically engineered subordinate 4th density surrogate species—including the Anuk (Anunnaki), Omicron, Alpha Draco, Niberians, and Greys—to physically enforce the occupation over the 3rd density human population they intended to subjugate.

The physical commencement of the parasitic takeover mirrored a SWAT team cutting power to a building. The Grey ET factions actively destroyed the Spirit Tree at Mt Meru, replacing it with a petrified stump. This catastrophic act instantly severed the primary energy supply across the entire Gateway-10 system, preventing indigenous species from mounting a defense and plunging the realm's operational frequency low enough for the 14-foot tall, low-vibrational parasitic entities to physically manifest on the land without becoming violently ill.
