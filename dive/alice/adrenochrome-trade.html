Adrenochrome Trade

Overview

The extraction, stockpiling, and consumption of Adrenochrome represents the central physical motivation for the parasitic occupation of the physical plane. Within the parameters of the 3rd density simulation on Gateway-10, the systemic harvesting of this biological fluid from human children is inexorably linked to both the energetic sustenance of negative entities and the absolute hierarchical control of human society. The adrenochrome trade dictates the biological engineering of the human vessel, the cyclical destruction of planetary civilizations, and the implementation of an unbreakable, blackmail-based compliance matrix that ensures the complicity of all top-tier human leadership.

Key Terminology

Adrenochrome

The highly prized physical fluid extracted from terrified, tortured children, utilized as the primary biological resource for parasitic species, sold souls, and demonic entities for consumption and ritualistic practices.

Loosh

The negative energetic frequency (fear, terror, suffering) harvested alongside adrenochrome, acting as vital energetic sustenance for demons and parasitic extraterrestrials.

Re-sets

Cyclical, planned, planet-wide extermination events designed to gather massive quantities of adrenochrome and loosh, followed by the repopulation of the realm using cloned entities.

D.U.M.B.S.

Deep Underground Military Bases where cloned children are grown in subterranean facilities for the express purpose of organ harvesting, torture, and sacrifice.

33rd Degree Freemasons

The highest tier of human collaborators managing the societal matrix; initiation demands the recorded sacrifice of a child to secure their absolute allegiance through blackmail.

Homo Sapiens

The specific human hominid vessel genetically selected and optimized by the parasites to produce the highest possible yield of adrenochrome per milliliter of blood.

Galactic Ancestral Alliance (G.A.A)

The benevolent extraterrestrial coalition responsible for dismantling the parasitic control mechanisms, halting the adrenochrome trade, and orchestrating the planetary liberation.

Core Revelations

The cultivation and trade of adrenochrome is not an incidental byproduct of the parasitic occupation; it is its foundational industry. The parasitic entities—comprising the Custodians, Anuk (Anunnaki), Omicron, Alpha Draco, and Greys—meticulously designed the modern human condition to maximize the production of this fluid.

Previous hominid iterations, such as Neanderthals, possessed larger craniums, higher intelligence, and greater physical durability, rendering them unsuitable for subjugation and mass harvesting. The parasites discontinued these genetic lines in favor of the Homo Sapiens vessel, which was explicitly engineered to be docile, malleable, and capable of generating maximum adrenochrome during trauma. This biological engineering is the foundational control mechanism that allows the adrenochrome trade to exist.

Furthermore, the operational hubs of this trade are deeply embedded beneath highly guarded planetary infrastructure. The Vatican, serving as the central headquarters for all parasitic factions, conceals 13 subterranean levels housing luxury slaughterhouses, cages for human children, and state-of-the-art "spare adrenochrome warehouses". The precision of this extraction process is absolute; not a single drop of adrenochrome is missed or wasted during any sacrificial event.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Blackmail Matrix and Societal Control The adrenochrome trade enforces strict hierarchical control over human society through extreme compromise. To ascend to positions of ultimate societal influence—whether as a knighted "Sir," a monarch, a high-ranking politician, a celebrity, or a judicial authority—an individual must become a 33rd Degree Freemason. The mandatory initiation rite requires the candidate to torture and murder a human child on camera. This documented atrocity serves as the "glue" of the parasitic power structure, ensuring that every individual in a position of societal authority is held hostage by irrefutable evidence of their crimes. This renders the entire global leadership class completely subservient to the parasitic agenda out of fear of public exposure and severe retaliation.

Cyclical Planetary Harvesting The parasites operate the adrenochrome trade on a millennia-long cycle. Re-sets are orchestrated every thousand years to execute a planet-wide harvest, representing the most significant event on the parasitic calendar. During these periods, the entire surface population—adults and children—is subjected to extreme torture, rape, and consumption to maximize the extraction of adrenochrome and loosh. To ensure the continuation of the trade, stem cells are extracted from children prior to their sacrifice. These cells are used to grow clones in D.U.M.B.S., producing a new "crop" of parentless orphans who are then distributed across the realm (e.g., via "Orphan Trains") to repopulate the cleared cities and initiate the next cultivation cycle.

Subterranean and Institutional Storage The logistics of the adrenochrome trade require vast, shielded infrastructure. Beyond the Vatican's centralized warehouses, high-level elites maintain personal reserves, such as the "Adrenochrome cellars" located in the basements of elite estates like Châteaux Don Perignon. Subterranean commercial infrastructure, such as the D.U.M.B.S. strategically positioned beneath major corporate outlets like Walmart (an anagram for "Mart-Law" or Martial Law), serve as localized containment, execution, and harvesting centers.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Symbiosis of Adrenochrome and Loosh The physical extraction of adrenochrome operates in parallel with the energetic extraction of Loosh. While adrenochrome serves as biological material, Loosh is the energetic sustenance required by the demonic entities that host "sold soul" human collaborators. Following a planetary Re-set, the immediate priority of the parasites is to feed these demons to prevent the collapse of their operational power structure. To accomplish this, the traumatized, catatonic human survivors of the Re-set are corralled into repurposed Lunatic Asylums, which function explicitly as 5,000-bed "Loosh Batteries"—massive energetic storage facilities designed to keep the demonic hierarchy fed while the next generation of adrenochrome-producing humans is bred.

Off-World Frequency Control The harvesting apparatus extends beyond the terrestrial surface. The Moon, operating as a holographic projection masking a negative extraterrestrial space station, was utilized as a command center for harvesting Loosh from Earth and 65 other enslaved worlds. The station actively broadcasted negative frequencies—particularly during lunar stages like the full moon—to induce psychological distress ("Lunatic" behavior) in the human population, thereby spiking the production of both Loosh and the internal neurochemical conditions necessary for high-quality adrenochrome.

Strategic Implications

The 8th Re-set and the Permanent Farm The ultimate strategic objective of the adrenochrome trade was the permanent, inescapable subjugation of the human species via the 8th Re-set, originally scheduled for 2019. This event was designed to lock a severely reduced population of 500 million humans (as documented on the now-destroyed Georgia Guidestones) into an eternal loop of "birth, torture, rape, [and] sacrifice". This would have established a permanent, enclosed factory-farm for adrenochrome and Loosh, forever terminating the spiritual progression of Taran (human) souls and cementing the parasites' victory over physical creation.

The EBS and the Collapse of the Matrix The intervention of the G.A.A has definitively halted the adrenochrome trade and terminated the parasitic occupation. However, the revelation of this trade is the mechanism that will shatter the engineered reality of the human population. The upcoming Emergency Broadcast System (EBS) will expose the unequivocal truth that every trusted institution and beloved societal figure—from royalty to children's television presenters—was complicit in the industrial-scale torture and harvesting of children. The psychological devastation of realizing that human history was managed by a blackmail-driven pedovore syndicate will induce total catatonia and psychological collapse in the 97% of the population designated as NPCs, initiating their physical evaporation via the EMF (Electro Magnetic Frequency) flash.
