Oopa Artifacts

Overview

The physical realm contains blatant physical evidence of high technological civilisations that existed prior to modern recorded history. These surviving items are categorized as Oopa (Out Of Place Artefacts), remnants deliberately left behind, fossilized, or missed during cyclical planetary Resets. Resets are catastrophic, pre-planned events orchestrated to terminate the existing population, harvest energy, and completely erase the advanced, free-energy civilization known as Tartaria. Because Oopas contradict the manufactured narrative of linear human evolution and primitive historical origins, they are actively suppressed, hidden in museum basements, or dismissed as anomalies. The existence of Oopas—ranging from fossilized modern items to free-energy mechanisms and giant skeletal remains—provides undeniable proof that the timeline of human progress has been repeatedly interrupted, suppressed, and inverted by parasitic forces.

Key Terminology

Oopa (Out Of Place Artefacts)

Blatant physical evidence and items left behind from high technological civilizations that existed before the current reset, completely contradicting the accepted historical narrative.

Resets

Pre-planned, devastating planetary events executed by parasitic forces to wipe out advanced civilizations, harvest human populations for energy, and reset societal knowledge back to a primitive, controllable state.

Tartaria

A globally advanced, pre-reset civilization characterized by resplendent architecture, crystalline healing temples, and the utilization of free electromagnetic energy, deliberately eradicated to enforce physical scarcity.

Energy Weapons

Advanced technology used during resets to alter molecular and subatomic cohesion, capable of turning physical matter (such as trees, people, or everyday objects) entirely to stone, or dustifying massive buildings.

Lattice Membrane Network

Also known as Ley Lines, this is the natural electromagnetic grid connecting energetic Nodal Points across the realm, historically utilized to power Tartarian architecture and transportation.

Atmospheric Condensers

Tartarian copper resonator technology affixed to early locomotives, enabling them to run purely on electromagnetic induction gathered from the lattice membrane network without the need for coal.

Core Revelations

The accepted historical timeline is a complete fabrication designed to obscure the reality of Resets and the advanced nature of the previous realm. True progression was not a slow climb from primitive apes to modern society, but rather a catastrophic downgrade from 9th density perfection to 3rd density control.

Major global institutions, such as the Smithsonian Institute and the Vatican, function as primary containment vaults for Oopas and definitive proof of pre-reset existence. When anomalous discoveries are made by the public, recovery teams deploy NDAs (Non-Disclosure Agreements) under threat of imprisonment and the forfeiture of assets to ensure absolute silence, spiriting the artifacts away to sealed vaults. Institutions operate on a rigid mandate: things that cannot be explained according to the false historical timeline are permanently hidden from public display.

The destruction or concealment of key Oopas was the true motive behind major historical disasters. The sinking of the Titanic, alongside eliminating opposition to the Federal Reserve, was specifically orchestrated to destroy highly sensitive cargo: a massive, high-energy Atlantean crystal and Tartarian Tuning Forks utilized for frequency-based construction.

World events were coordinated to eliminate the memory of these artifacts and the technology they represent. World War 1 was designed and timed specifically to exterminate reset survivors between the ages of 15 and 55. These individuals retained dangerous old-world knowledge regarding true geography, natural remedies, and the reality of the pre-reset realm, and their eradication severed the oral transmission of true history.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

Oopas manifest in numerous forms, each exposing the mechanics of the previous civilization and the highly advanced weapons used to destroy it.

  • Fossilized Objects: Artifacts such as a fossilised hat and a fossilised book demonstrate the deployment of ancient Energy Weapons during a reset. These weapons do not utilize nuclear radiation, but rather emit frequencies that fuse incompatible materials and alter subatomic cohesion, instantly turning objects and biological entities to solid stone. This mechanism is a direct cousin to Cold Fusion.

  • Giant Skeletons: During the post-reset expansion of the American railroad system, workers sliced through hills that were actually ancient burial mounds, uncovering over 10,000 skeletons of Giants. These physical beings ranged from 12 feet to over 35 meters in height, with some individual human femurs measuring 30 meters long. The Smithsonian immediately confiscated these remains to prevent the collapse of the evolutionary narrative.

  • Harmonic Architecture Tools: In Tartarian times, massive edifices were not constructed with physical labor, but were sculpted using advanced Tuning Forks. This technology manipulated sound and light frequencies to turn hard rock, such as granite and andesite, into a weightless putty that could be imprinted with vibrational molds and effortlessly lifted. The legacy of this technology was briefly utilized by Edward Leedskalnin in the early 20th century; using old-world knowledge, glass bottles wrapped in copper wire, and a magnetic crank, he independently cut, moved, and balanced 1,100 tons of limestone to build his monument, proving the laws of weight and leverage were frequency-based.

  • Self-Powering Locomotives: Early trains featured advanced domed copper resonators on their boilers, mirroring the domes on Tartarian buildings. Woven with copper wire in Fibonacci sequences, these Atmospheric Condensers utilized electromagnetic induction to superheat water simply by traveling through the varying magnetic fields of the underlying Ley Lines. These machines increased performance and reduced coal consumption by up to 60%. In 1887, the Consolidated Coal Company ordered all such devices stripped from locomotives and permanently melted down into molten metal to force total societal reliance on harvested coal.

  • Mudflood Obliteration: Building obliteration during resets utilized soil liquefaction and targeted frequency weapons. Massive stone and steel buildings were dustified into particulate matter, leaving behind half-buried structures and inexplicable rubble that the newly seeded populations simply built their false histories upon.

Broader Context and Interconnections

Oopas cannot be understood in isolation; they are the surviving physical fragments of an intricate, globally interconnected system that was violently dismantled.

Following a Reset, the physical landscape was wiped clean of free-energy technology, leaving only the grandest architectural shells. To repopulate this altered environment, Orphan Trains distributed cloned children, grown in deep underground military bases (D.U.M.B.S.), across the realm. Because these children lacked any historical memory, they readily accepted the false evolutionary explanations for the majestic, repurposed buildings surrounding them.

The psychological trauma of witnessing a reset necessitated the creation of massive Lunatic Asylums. These facilities were not originally built for mental healthcare; they were majestic Tartarian buildings repurposed immediately after the reset to house shell-shocked survivors who had witnessed the slaughter of the realm. By consolidating the traumatized, these asylums functioned as massive Loosh Batteries, generating the negative emotional energy required by the parasitic entities to sustain their control over the physical density.

The false doctrine of Evolution—positing that humanity crawled from the ocean to slowly develop tools—was fabricated by 33rd degree Freemasons specifically to explain away the existence of biological variety, hide the true origins of Oopas, and mask the truth of genetic lab-creation. Progression does not naturally flow from a stone wheel to a mechanical cog; the wheel itself was a primitive downgrade forced upon a civilization that previously traveled and built using levitation and pure electromagnetic resonance.
