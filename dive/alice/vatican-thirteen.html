Overview

Vatican Thirteen is the subterranean command nexus, energetic control center, and multi-faction power base positioned beneath the surface structure of the Vatican. While the surface edifice consists exclusively of repurposed Tartarian architecture, the functional core of the facility resides in a vast underground complex extending exactly 13 levels below ground. Functioning as a centralized operational hub within the broader network of parasitic control mechanisms, the complex was engineered to consolidate hostile parasitic entities and host species under a single structure. The 13 subterranean levels house luxury slaughterhouses, state-of-the-art entertainment, cages full of children, adrenochrome warehouses, and independent interdimensional portals designated for spatial and soul transit.

Key Terminology

Vatican Thirteen

The 13-level subterranean command and harvesting facility situated beneath the surface Vatican structure, serving as a consolidated headquarters, resource stockpile, and portal nexus for competing parasitic factions.

Repurposed Tartarian Architecture

Physical structures and artistic works inherited from prior advanced civilizations that were expropriated by parasitic forces and falsely attributed to figureheads such as Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci.

Custodians

The primary architect entity group that established and managed the 13 subterranean levels to keep distrustful parasitic species and entities consolidated under a single physical installation.

Smithsonian Institution

The premier North American archaeological entity operating as the Vatican of archaeology, maintaining subterranean vaults restricted exclusively to parasites and elite bloodline members.

Subterranean Portals

Specialized dimensional transit mechanisms integrated into the subterranean levels, providing unshared travel gateways for specific factions and facilitating the redistribution of souls across the realm.

Core Revelations

The surface structures of the Vatican mask a completely inverted subterranean reality. In previous historical cycles, the surface building existed as a castle, and its current physical presentation consists entirely of stolen Tartarian stonework and art. Legendary historical attributions—including names such as Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci—are fraudulent labels placed over older pre-existing works to obscure true historical origin.

The subterranean facility was constructed to solve a critical operational problem among the ruling parasitic forces: absolute mutual distrust. Independent parasitic groups—including Omicron, Alpha Draco, the Custodians, Anuk, Greys, and Demons—were unable to share unmonitored space or transit routes. To prevent open conflict and maintain control over the realm, the Custodians centralized all faction headquarters within one building, allocating distinct subterranean levels to each group.

An identical structural model governs archaeological control via the Smithsonian Institution. Recognized as the archaeological counterpart to the Vatican, the Smithsonian maintains sealed subterranean vaults beneath its public floors. Access below public sight lines is strictly prohibited to ordinary human beings, reserved exclusively for non-human parasites and verified bloodline lineages.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The subterranean infrastructure of Vatican Thirteen operates across 13 specialized subterranean levels, engineered for resource extraction, entity accommodation, and portal navigation. Each level operates as a self-contained headquarters for a specific ruling faction or entity class.

Level Allocation and Facility Features

Because none of the ruling parasitic factions trusted one another, each group maintained an isolated level equipped with its own dedicated infrastructure:

  • Omicron Level — Dedicated operational headquarters for the Omicron entity group.
  • Alpha Draco Level — Isolated command level for the Alpha Draco faction.
  • Custodians Level — Administrative and oversight level operated by the Custodians.
  • Anuk Level — Functional headquarters assigned to the Anuk (Anunnaki).
  • Greys Level — Technical and navigational level designated for the Greys.
  • Demonic Chambers — Specialized levels assigned to high-tier Demons and their consorts.

Across these 13 levels, vast spaces are dedicated to physical and energetic harvesting. The facility contains luxury slaughterhouses and high-end entertainment quarters for resident entities and their consorts. To maintain continuous operational stockpiles, lower levels house spare cages filled with captive children alongside extensive adrenochrome storage warehouses.

Portal Mechanics and Incarnational Management

A primary technical function of the subterranean complex is interdimensional and regional transit. Each faction possessed portal access within its designated level, allowing entities to travel independently without sharing transit points with rival groups.

One of these subterranean levels contains the primary portal array responsible for managing human soul redistribution. Following physical death, human souls pass through the sun for automated re-formatting and enter the portal array beneath the Vatican. The Greys utilize specialized incarnational technology within this level to oversee the transit process. Grey operators escort the re-formatted soul directly to its designated target country, where the soul exercises limited selection over mothers currently giving birth.

Archaeological Parallel: The Smithsonian Vaults

The structural design of Vatican Thirteen is replicated in North America through the Smithsonian Institution. Operating as the Vatican of archaeology, the Smithsonian functions under identical access protocols. While public upper floors display sanitized exhibits, the vast vaults beneath the facility remain air-sealed and hidden from public view. Entry into the subterranean levels of the Smithsonian is strictly restricted to parasites and bloodline members, ensuring that out-of-place physical evidence, giant remains, and historical artifacts recovered from excavation projects are permanently sequestered.

Broader Context and Interconnections

Vatican Thirteen functions as the primary nexus connecting subterranean harvesting, interdimensional transit, and historical deception. The portal technology embedded within the 13 levels links directly to the solar soul-processing cycle and the Grey incarnational management system. By controlling the exit point of re-formatted souls beneath the Vatican, parasitic factions dictate global birth distribution and prevent soul groups or twin flames from reuniting.

Furthermore, the operational link between the Vatican and the Smithsonian Institution demonstrates a coordinated global mechanism for physical and historical suppression. Where the Vatican controls religious, energetic, and soul-transit vectors, the Smithsonian manages physical archaeology and material history. Both installations rely on multi-level subterranean vaults to isolate operational truths from the public surface world.

Strategic Implications

The centralized architecture of Vatican Thirteen established long-term operational stability for parasitic control over the realm. By containing Omicron, Alpha Draco, Custodians, Anuk, Greys, and Demons inside a single 13-level structure, the Custodians mitigated inter-faction warfare while ensuring that loosh harvesting, adrenochrome warehousing, and child exploitation operated at maximum efficiency.

The enforcement of absolute secrecy regarding subterranean levels—both at the Vatican and the Smithsonian—guaranteed the preservation of the surface narrative. By cloaking stolen Tartarian stonework under false artistic attributions like Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci, and by locking physical artifacts in sealed underground vaults, global controllers successfully severed humanity from its authentic past while maintaining continuous control over the incarnational loop.
