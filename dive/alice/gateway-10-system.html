Gateway-10 System

Overview

Within the true, suppressed reality of physical existence, the manufactured illusion of a vast, heliocentric universe hides the existence of the Gateway-10 System. Far from being a series of spherical planets orbiting suns in a dark vacuum, the cosmos is a localized, horizontal physical plane. Gateway-10 represents this central physical plane of existence, comprising 178 interconnected worlds operating on a massive Toroid field of energy. Originally orchestrated by the Source of All Creation as a perfect manifestation environment, the central node of this gateway was hijacked by 4th-density Custodians and their engineered parasitic forces. By physically partitioning the known lands and destroying the central energetic architecture, these controllers plunged the core of Gateway-10 into a suppressed, low-frequency matrix, threatening the structural and operational integrity of the entire 178-world system.

Key Terminology

Gateway-10 System

The central physical plane of existence encompassing 178 interconnected, horizontal flat worlds.

Toroid field

The primary energy vortex and electromagnetic system powering the Gateway, which centers exactly on the realm currently inhabited by humanity.

Realm-3 (The Known Lands)

The currently inhabited, heavily partitioned segment of the flat Earth, forcefully carved out of the original central realm via the fake Antarctic ice wall.

Realm-2

The original, vastly wider physical plane located at the very center of Gateway-10 before the parasitic inversion and partitioning.

Realm-1

An adjacent flat world; its outer ice boundary is physically joined to the landmass that manufactured science falsely labels as the distant planet Mars.

Spirit Tree (Mt Meru / Hyperborea)

The enormously powerful energetic and structural core present at the center of all realms; the primary tree powering Gateway-10 was destroyed by parasites.

Source of All Creation

A vast, non-physical etheric supercomputer and intellect that orchestrated the multidimensional master plan to build Gateway-10 for the physical progression of souls.

Custodians

Originally the benevolent caretakers of the physical plane who betrayed the Source, initiating the hostile takeover of Gateway-10 to claim it for themselves.

Core Revelations

The defining truth of physical cosmology is that the entire universe known to humanity is merely one partitioned fragment situated perfectly in the center of the Gateway-10 System. Gateway-10 is the physical plane of existence itself, supporting 178 adjacent, flat worlds separated by physical ice barriers. Because Realm-3 resides at the absolute center of the Toroid field, it is historically and mechanically the most powerful location on the entire Gateway, making it the primary target for parasitic subjugation.

The original, high-vibrational physical plane was flawless, but it suffered a hostile inversion. The Custodians, seeking absolute autonomy, plotted over millennia to hijack Gateway-10. They created numerous negative species, such as the Anunnaki and Grey ETs, to execute this takeover. Following the inversion of the center, the parasites expanded their control, harvesting Loosh and manipulating frequencies across 65 other worlds within the Gateway-10 network, though the human realm remained their paramount asset.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The operational mechanics of Gateway-10 rely on interconnected energetic grids, central power nodes, and sub-plane resource networks:

  • The Severing of the Toroid Power Source: To conquer the highest-frequency central realm, the parasites executed a strategy akin to cutting the power to a building. Grey ETs utilized advanced technology to destroy the primary Spirit Tree located at the North Pole. By replacing this massive energetic generator with a petrified stump, the power and frequency of the entire Toroid field dropped instantly across all 178 worlds.

  • Frequency Suppression for Physical Occupation: The destruction of the central Spirit Tree was mechanically necessary for the occupation of Gateway-10. Originally, the ambient frequencies of the central realm were far too high for 4th-density parasites to physically withstand. Dampening the Toroid field allowed these hostile entities to safely disembark their craft and operate on the ground.

  • Sub-Plane Lubrication and Resources: The physical foundation of Gateway-10 is continuous and unified beneath the surface. Oil is not the decomposed remains of dinosaurs; it is a natural, highly advanced technological lubricant that flows freely under the entire sub-plane of the Gateway. It is not restricted by surface boundaries like ice walls or the Firmament, demonstrating the massive, connected horizontal architecture of the 178 worlds. Similarly, heavy metals and crystals were purposefully placed throughout the Gateway's subterranean layout to serve as an electromagnetic mechanism for the Lattice Membrane Network (Ley Lines).

Broader Context and Interconnections

The reality of Gateway-10 directly interconnects with the manufactured lies of the Flat Earth and the Ice Wall. To keep human consciousness trapped and oblivious to the other 177 worlds, terrestrial agents such as 33rd-degree Freemasons rigidly enforce the heliocentric globe model. However, knowledge within the control matrix is highly compartmentalized; even top-tier Freemasons only know that the Earth is a flat, enclosed plane. They remain entirely ignorant of Gateway-10's full scope and the adjacent 177 realms existing beyond the ice walls.

This massive cosmological deception acts in tandem with 3rd Density Overlays, utilizing technology projected by Sky-Net-1 to hide ultra-high-frequency Tartarian and Crystalline architecture built upon the Gateway's major Nodes.

Strategic Implications

The parasitic occupation of Gateway-10 nearly culminated in total, irreversible systemic failure. The controllers planned an 8th and final reset that would reduce the human population of the central realm to 500 million, trapping them in an eternal loop of torture and sacrifice. Had this succeeded, the spiritual and energetic collapse of the center would have caused the immediate fall of the entire Gateway.

The remaining 177 worlds, observing the crown jewel of physical existence fall completely, would have either surrendered or been destroyed by the influx of new parasitic species. This would have equated to the ultimate defeat of the Source of All Creation, wasting billions of years of multidimensional engineering. The intervention by the Galactic Ancestral Alliance (G.A.A) was strategically mandatory to seize control of the simulation, halt the final reset, and ultimately restore the central Toroid field of Gateway-10 to its original, perfect functionality.
