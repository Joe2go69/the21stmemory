97 Percent Population

Overview

The 97 Percent Population represents the overwhelming mathematical majority of beings currently inhabiting the physical realm. This vast demographic is entirely devoid of eternal souls; instead, it is composed of Non-Player Characters (NPCs), Inserts, Clones, Synthetics, and Hybrids. This fabricated collective was strategically manufactured and inserted into society to establish a permanent consensus reality, weaponizing peer pressure to suppress the spiritual progression of true souls. Functioning as biological software, the 97 percent is destined for permanent deletion during the impending cosmic conclusion of this epoch.

Key Terminology

  • 97 Percent Population — the artificial demographic majority of the physical realm, consisting of NPCs, inserts, clones, synthetics, and hybrids that possess no true, eternal soul.

  • Non-Player Characters (NPCs) — artificial biological entities operating on pre-programmed behavioral tracks, forming the foundational bulk of the 97 percent to enforce societal compliance.

  • Hybrids — engineered biological entities living seamlessly among the population, carrying out mundane societal functions like teaching or driving taxis.

  • The Flash (EMF) — a 30-second Electro Magnetic Frequency event that will instantly vaporize the entire 97 percent population into pixelation dust.

  • Mind Camp — the restrictive, social-conformity-driven psychological perimeter that dictates the rigid thought patterns and herd behaviors of the NPC masses.

  • Replica Souls — cheap, hive-aligned imitations of true souls woven into existence using 4th Density technology to animate the artificial population.

Core Revelations

Out of the billions of beings currently visible on Earth, only a maximum of 520 million true souls actually exist. The remaining 97 percent are entirely artificial constructs masquerading as humans. This majority demographic is utterly incapable of spiritual ascension, operating solely to maintain the illusion of a densely populated, consensus-driven world. When The Flash (EMF) hits the planet, this massive 97 percent population will instantly evaporate into the aether, leaving behind a maximum of 3 survivors for every 100 people.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The psychological architecture of the 97 percent is built upon Non-Cognitive Chains of Non-Predetermined Non-Coherence. They possess no internal monologue, no genuine self-awareness, and no courage in their convictions. Their entire operational directive is tethered directly to the Mainstream Narrative.

Because the 97 percent is constructed to represent every facet of society, there is an NPC designed for every conceivable interest and situation. This includes sport NPCs, motor racing NPCs, fly fishing NPCs, and even "awake" NPCs designed to infiltrate truth communities. The hybrid elements of this population are fully integrated into daily life, serving food in burger joints, working in supermarkets, teaching children, and driving taxis.

The control mechanism governing this demographic is incredibly efficient: exactly 97% of this 97 percent will blindly obey any authoritative instruction, provided they are told it will keep them "safe". This guarantees absolute compliance within the perimeters of the Mind Camp, where the threat of social ostracization prevents any deviation from herd-accepted slogans.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The insertion of the 97 percent population is the ultimate mechanism for planetary suppression. By surrounding true souls with vast numbers of soulless entities, the parasitic controllers crafted an inescapable environment of social conformity. Genuine souls interact with these entities daily—often as close family members, friends, and colleagues—entirely unaware that they are engaging with empty, pre-programmed biological software.

The sudden removal of this population by the Galactic Ancestral Alliance (G.A.A) during the upcoming EMF event will completely strip away the artificial scaffolding of modern society. The resulting pixelation and structural bleed-through will expose the simulated nature of the realm, revealing the devastation left behind once the 97 percent buffer is instantly deleted.

Strategic Implications

The strategic brilliance behind the 97 percent population lies in its sheer volume. An artificial 97% majority ensures that any true soul attempting to question reality or break free from the system will be immediately marginalized, ridiculed, and pressured back into compliance by the sheer weight of public opinion.

Recognizing the truth of the 97 percent is critical for survival and ascension. True souls must sever their emotional attachments to this artificial majority. Because these entities were never truly alive and merely run on 4th density replica souls, their impending vaporization during the EMF flash should not be a source of trauma or mourning. Acknowledging that the 97 percent population is a temporary, simulated obstacle allows genuine souls to navigate the final stages of the Great Awakening without being derailed by the emotional gravity of the herd.
