Overview

The Amnesia Vortex operates as the central cognitive and spiritual erasure tool within a vast, interwoven network of Control Mechanisms designed to imprison human souls on the physical plane. Established by negative extraterrestrial entities and their created subordinates following an initial betrayal over 178,000 years ago, these systems ensure the continuous recycling of spiritual energy, or Loosh, and the biological harvesting of Adrenochrome. The vortex functions alongside multi-layered environmental, societal, and biological suppressants to trap souls in an artificial 3rd density matrix, enforcing total compliance, historical amnesia, and absolute separation from the Source of Creation. The architecture of this control grid dictates every facet of human perception, utilizing holographic technology, chemical biological limiters, and engineered populations to maintain the illusion of a natural, evolutionary existence.

Core Revelations

The deceptive "Bright Light" witnessed during the near-death experience is not a divine presence; it is the Sun, which functions as an etheric portal managing the time between physical vessels. Upon entering this portal, the Amnesia Vortex strips the soul of its previous memories. This entire soul-redistribution operation is physically anchored deep beneath the Earth's surface, specifically within one of the 13 subterranean levels below the Vatican.

Within 15 to 20 minutes of physical death, the re-formatted soul is forcefully escorted by Grey ETs to a predetermined geographic location to incarnate into a newborn vessel, often just as the umbilical cord is cut. This rapid, forced recycling explicitly prevents Twin Flames from reuniting, ensuring maximum isolation and psychological vulnerability. Crucially, the Amnesia Vortex was successfully deactivated in 2019, which is why an increasing number of children are now recalling accurate past-life memories and mission directives without systemic erasure.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The overarching system of entrapment relies on interlocking layers of suppression:

  1. Environmental and Holographic Suppression: The sky and cosmos as understood by humanity are weaponized illusions. What is perceived as the Moon is a negative ET space station and frequency control center. It is artificially illuminated by a holographic generator known as Planet Venus (the "Bright Morning Star" or "Lucifer"), which casts light onto the lunar structure. During a full moon, this station broadcasts negative frequencies designed to destabilize human cognition.

  2. Biological and Chemical Limiters: The physical vessel is heavily targeted to disable the Pineal Gland and sever inter-dimensional signal reception. Vaping and pharmaceutical interventions deliver nano-particles, heavy metals (Nickel, Lead, Chromium), and synthetic endocrine disruptors directly to the brain. This calcifies the pineal gland, blocks the Chakras, and introduces micro A.I. programs into the bloodstream to execute behavioral nudges.

  3. The 3 Strings of Psychological Entrapment: The matrix is secured through three primary cognitive bonds:

    • Religion — The worship of false gods, effectively redirecting spiritual devotion to Satan and subjugating cognition.

    • Finance — The pursuit of meaningless currency, blinding individuals to their exploitation.

    • Perceived Knowledge — A false, rigidly controlled academic and historical curriculum heavily curated by 33rd-degree Freemasons to ensure foundational truths regarding Tartaria, the flat earth, and the true origins of humanity are suppressed.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The control grid extends far beyond immediate soul-recycling into multi-generational cycles of planetary destruction known as Re-sets. Approximately every 1,000 years, the realm is deliberately depopulated to harvest ultimate quantities of Loosh and Adrenochrome, eradicating advanced Old World technological civilizations. Following the slaughter, the Earth is repopulated using laboratory-grown clones transported via Orphan Trains.

To sustain the demonic hierarchies immediately following a reset, buildings designated as Lunatic Asylums were constructed to warehouse traumatized survivors, serving as 5,000-bed "Loosh Batteries" to feed entities before the new population matured.

To enforce the fabricated reality of these new populations, the Custodians and their subordinates deploy Non-Player Characters (NPCs). NPCs constitute 97% of the total population. Powered by 4th-density replica hive-aligned souls, NPCs lack self-awareness or internal monologues and function strictly to enforce the mainstream narrative, creating an inescapable societal "Mind Camp" that regulates the behavior of genuine Taran and ET souls.

Strategic Implications

The operational infrastructure of the Control Mechanisms is currently facing a total, terminal collapse engineered by the Galactic Ancestral Alliance (G.A.A.). Because the Amnesia Vortex is deactivated, the generational cycle of ignorance is permanently broken.

As the final dismantling occurs, humanity will undergo severe Scare Events, culminating in a fake alien invasion generated by Project Bluebeam holographics, designed to trigger the final collapse of the NPCs and those hopelessly tethered to the 3 Strings. Ultimately, the Projection Dome will be switched off, causing the fake sky to pixelate and fall, revealing the true white cosmos. This will be followed immediately by a 30-second Electro Magnetic Frequency (EMF) Flash that will permanently vaporize the 97% NPC population, leaving only a small, awakened remnant of genuine souls on Earth to inherit the restored realm.
