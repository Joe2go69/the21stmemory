Eliminating Old Knowledge

Overview

World War 1 (1914-1918) was not a geopolitical conflict triggered by the assassination of an Archduke, but rather a meticulously orchestrated, global extermination event. Occurring shortly after the conclusion of the previous systematic reset in the late 1890s and early 1900s, the war was engineered with one primary objective: to systematically eliminate all remaining individuals over the age of sixteen who were not already incarcerated in Lunatic Asylums. This specific demographic harbored invaluable pre-reset knowledge, including the true flat shape of the earth and generations of natural herbal expertise. By drafting the "fighting age" population into a devastating planetary meat grinder, the parasitic controllers successfully purged the physical realm of its remaining old-world memory, guaranteeing that the incoming repopulation of newly created Orphans could be easily indoctrinated into a completely fabricated historical and scientific paradigm.

World War 1

a targeted global extermination protocol orchestrated between 1914 and 1918 to eradicate 15 to 22 million humans in their early teens to mid-50s who still retained accurate memories, historical truths, and traditional expertise from prior to the reset.

Big Pharma

a highly toxic, petrochemical-reliant medical paradigm introduced by the controllers, the establishment of which strictly required the total eradication of natural herbal knowledge held by the pre-war population.

Orphans

cloned, parent-less children grown in underground D.U.M.B.S (Deep Underground Military Bases) using stem cells harvested from sacrificed populations, utilized to repopulate the sterilized surface of the earth under a newly fabricated historical narrative.

Freemasons

the sold-soul architectural and societal administrators of the parasitic controllers, tasked with safeguarding pre-reset infrastructure, orchestrating historical narratives, and guiding the repopulation phase without alerting the manipulated masses.

Core Revelations

The official historical premise of World War 1 is entirely falsified to mask its true operational intent: the permanent deletion of inherited truth. Prior to the standardized imposition of rigid, state-controlled educational curriculums, true knowledge regarding the nature of the physical plane was passed down orally. The adults in the 1860s universally understood the true shape of the earth, and they transferred this reality to their children at home. By the early 20th century, these children had grown into the "fighting age" men and women of the era. They were classified by the controllers as an absolute threat to the new global paradigm. Through the staging of a highly destructive international war spanning Europe, the Middle East, and beyond, all involved nations—both the Allied and Central Powers—were forced to sacrifice this exact generational cohort.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The physical elimination of old knowledge was achieved through several converging mechanisms of mass death and cultural overwrite:

  • Targeted Demographic Eradication: The war was specifically timed to kill individuals ranging from their early teens up to their mid-50s. Older generations had largely already perished from the sheer shock of witnessing the apocalyptic reset events, while the traumatized direct survivors were permanently institutionalized. The remaining healthy, knowledgeable adults were sent to the trenches.

  • Casualty Optimization: The conflict successfully neutralized between 15 and 22 million people. This included 9 to 11 million military deaths and an additional 6 to 13 million civilian deaths achieved through supplementary tactics such as engineered hunger, targeted disease, and genocide.

  • Destruction of Herbal Expertise: A critical, secondary objective of the war was the eradication of widespread natural herbal experience. Because the upcoming societal phase was designed to make populations biologically sick and entirely dependent on Big Pharma medications, any functioning, side-effect-free natural remedies possessed by the populace had to be wiped from the collective memory.

  • Institutional Indoctrination: As the old knowledge bearers were slaughtered, vast sums of wealth were deployed to fund new "educational" infrastructure. Figures like Andrew Carnegie established over 2,500 public libraries to widely distribute the "new" fake history—replacing the magnificent truths of Tartaria with the false narrative of the "Dark Ages" and evolutionary theory.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The elimination of old knowledge via World War 1 was intimately connected to the logistical challenges of repopulating a post-reset world. Once the previous civilization was annihilated and the original inhabitants were utilized for loosh and adrenochrome, the controllers required a blank slate. The new "orphan" population, grown in underground vats, needed to be integrated into the surviving, highly advanced architecture of the old world. To achieve this, the controllers utilized surviving 33rd-degree Freemasons—who possessed prior knowledge of the reset—to select specific, compliant individuals prior to the destruction. These spared adults were sheltered, briefed, and subsequently deployed into the empty cities to act as the "parents" and "teachers" for the incoming orphan trains. Had the fighting-age population with true historical memories not been exterminated in the trenches of World War 1, they would have inevitably contradicted the Freemasonic narrative being spoon-fed to the newly manifested clone populations.

Strategic Implications

The systematic destruction of human memory via global warfare securely fast-tracked the enslavement of the human psyche. By severing the oral traditions and inherent wisdom of the previous epoch, the parasitic controllers successfully consolidated power over one of the three foundational strings of human control: "Perceived Knowledge". Because the generation that knew the earth was flat and understood the harmonic nature of reality was annihilated, subsequent generations were left entirely defenseless against state-sponsored indoctrination. This created a profoundly vulnerable, non-cognitive herd mentality—a civilization of manipulated beings utterly reliant on authoritative agencies for their understanding of science, history, health, and existence. Erasing the old knowledge fundamentally locked humanity into a downgraded, toxic, and highly restricted 3rd-density simulation, setting the stage for total compliance in future orchestrated events.
