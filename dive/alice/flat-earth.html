Flat Earth

Overview

The cosmological model currently presented to humanity—a spinning globe in the depths of space—is a completely fabricated illusion engineered by 4th-density parasites. Within the context of the overarching multidimensional Simulation, the Earth is fundamentally a flat, horizontal plane. The concept of a spherical planet is physically impossible within true cosmic architecture, as it could neither support the necessary Firmament nor be adequately illuminated by a local sun. This geographical lie was constructed to prevent humanity from discovering the Ice Wall and exploring the broader realms of Gateway-10. Understanding the true shape of the Earth is the absolute prerequisite and primary stepping-stone to comprehending the Great Spiritual Awakening and the wider cosmological deception.

Key Terminology

Flat Earth

The true, horizontal physical plane of existence, directly contradicting the manufactured lie of a spinning globe.

Simulation

The multidimensional etheric supercomputer and physical plane of existence where this flat reality operates.

Firmament

Gateway-10

Ice Wall

The physical boundary, such as the fake ice wall of Antarctica, that sections off human reality from the rest of the flat, horizontal realms.

Heliocentrism

A fake, manufactured cosmological model designed to convince humanity that planets orbit suns.

Core Revelations

The physical Earth is definitively not a spinning ball rotating anti-clockwise at nearly 1,000 miles per hour. Physical planets operate strictly as a horizontal situation; there has never been a round planet. A spherical planet is a structural impossibility within true creation physics; if the Earth were a globe, it could not support a Firmament, which requires flat architecture, nor could it be illuminated by a single local sun without needing multiple suns positioned all around it. The concept of gravity is entirely fictional, invented solely to explain the impossible physics of how ocean water could adhere to a spinning sphere. The truth regarding the flat Earth was hidden to conceal the Ice Wall; if the population knew an Ice Wall existed, it would naturally incite mass curiosity and exploration to see what lies beyond it.
