Control Mechanisms

Overview

The Parasitic Takeover of the physical plain of existence was orchestrated through an elaborate, multi-layered framework of multidimensional Control Mechanisms designed to permanently enslave the Taran human souls within Gateway-10 (the Known Lands). Initiated by the Custodians—former 12th-density caretakers who betrayed the Source of all Creation out of greed for autonomy—this takeover required the methodical subversion of planetary energy systems, the suppression of natural frequencies, and the total manipulation of human consciousness. By deploying highly advanced Density Suppression, false Overlays, and creating vast populations of NPCs (Non-Player Characters), the parasites established a dystopian 3rd-density simulation. This matrix is strictly governed by Three Strings—Religion, Finance, and Perceived Knowledge—which act as absolute psychological boundaries, ensuring humanity remains asleep and endlessly harvested for Loosh and Adrenochrome across repetitive, genocidal Resets.

Key Terminology

Custodians

Originally 12th-density, highly beautiful caretakers of the physical plain who instigated the inversion by plotting to take possession of creation, ultimately falling to 4th density as negativity altered their vibration and physical appearance.

Density Suppression

A technological process acting like a "dimmer dial" that lowers the vibrational frequency of an environment (e.g., from 9th density down to 3rd density), rendering higher-dimensional crystalline structures invisible to lower-density observation and dampening positive energies.

Overlays

Projected frequencies utilized to obscure unmoveable benevolent architecture and project fake environmental constructs, such as the illusion of distant stars and space.

Amnesia Vortex

Technology housed within the Sun and portal systems beneath the Vatican that forcefully pulls souls inward upon death, erasing their memories before immediate reincarnation into a new vessel to prevent spiritual accumulation of wisdom.

NPCs

Soulless, 4th-density created entities that comprise 97% of the earthly population. They are hard-wired into the mainstream narrative, recycled rather than reincarnated, and serve to enforce social conformity.

Baphomet Power Pylons

Parasitic infrastructure designed to superficially transport electrical power while covertly harvesting the natural, positive electromagnetic energy backed-up along the Lattice Membrane Network (Ley Lines).

The Three Strings

The primary psychological control tethers binding humanity to the matrix: Religion (belief in false gods), Finance (the pursuit of fake monetary value), and Perceived Knowledge (indoctrinated fake history and science).

Core Revelations

The Parasitic Takeover fundamentally required the destruction of Gateway-10’s power source. The parasites utilized created negative species—including the Anuk (Anunnaki), Grey ET, Omicron, and Alpha Draco—to physically sever the Spirit Tree (Mt Meru / Northern Rock / Hyperborea), replacing it with a petrified stump. This action instantly limited power across the gateway and vastly reduced the realm's frequency, allowing the 4th-density parasites to physically walk upon the Earth without being destroyed by its formerly high, benevolent vibrations.

To sustain this occupation, the parasites manufactured a continuous cycle of suffering and ignorance. Evolution, gravity, and the globe earth are entirely fabricated concepts engineered by 33rd-degree Freemasons to hide the true, flat, and enclosed nature of the realm, which is bordered by an Ice Wall and covered by a Firmament. The ultimate mechanism of control is the periodic Reset—a planetary culling occurring roughly every 1,000 years, where adults are systematically tortured, raped, and sacrificed to generate extreme Loosh and Adrenochrome. Society is then repopulated using cloned orphans grown in underground facilities (D.U.M.B.S.), who are educated into a newly fabricated historical narrative by surviving, compromised Freemasons.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

  • Physical and Environmental Confinement: The sky above is an elaborate fake. A Projection Dome sits inside the true Firmament, utilizing Black Void Plasma (technology sourced from the powerful Niberian species) to render the sky black. Space debris and asteroids are mere projections, sometimes weaponized via portals to deliver targeted biological destruction. To further control the physical plane, the parasites encase natural Nodal Points in concrete and tarmac, lowering the density so that newly designed, austere 3rd-density architecture can be built directly over the footings of indestructible, highly vibrating Crystalline Temples.

  • Biological and Chemical Subjugation: Humanity is subjected to relentless biological warfare to block spiritual abilities. Modern "vaping" was heavily pushed onto the youth to deliver heavy metals (Nickel, Lead, Chromium), nanoparticles, and synthetic polymers directly into the bloodstream. These toxins bypass digestion to calcify the Pineal Gland, blunt inter-dimensional signal reception, and introduce micro A.I. programs that facilitate NPC-like behavioral nudging. Furthermore, supermarket meat is entirely fabricated; it is grown in liquid vats rather than harvested from living animals, disconnecting the human vessel from natural life-force.

  • Societal and Psychological Enclosures: The 97% NPC population functions as a "Mind Camp"—an aggressive perimeter of social conformity. NPCs possess no internal monologue or self-awareness; their thought avenues are pre-laid and activated by mainstream media, TV (e.g., programming men to aspire to be "Homer Simpson"), and the education system. Through peer pressure and the fear of ostracization, NPCs keep genuine souls from questioning the boundaries of the Three Strings. Additionally, philanthropists like Andrew Carnegie were utilized to fund libraries and educational systems solely to spread fake history and science far and wide, ensuring "Perceived Knowledge" remained absolute.

  • Spiritual and Afterlife Traps: The Vatican sits atop 13 subterranean levels housing demons, portals, and Adrenochrome warehouses. Here, souls are processed immediately after death. The Amnesia Vortex draws souls into the light of the Sun, wiping their 178,000 years of cosmic memories. The Grey ETs then utilize technology to escort the erased soul directly into a new infant vessel at the exact moment of birth, guaranteeing no soul family members (Twin Flames) ever reunite or escape the incarnational loop. Frequency Fences are also deployed to trap the consciousness even during sleep, preventing astral escape.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Control Mechanisms are deeply tied to the erasure of Tartaria and the old world. The "Dark Ages" never existed; they are a fabricated historical buffer used to explain the sudden shift from the resplendent, free-energy civilization of Tartaria to the harsh, austere era of the Industrial Revolution. The parasites methodically downgraded humanity's aesthetic and technological surroundings to lower expectations, utilizing 33rd-degree Freemason architects to build depressing, "Battle-Ship Grey" infrastructure. World War 1 was orchestrated specifically as a Control Mechanism to eradicate 15-22 million adults who retained old-world knowledge or herbal expertise from before the last Reset, ensuring the new, cloned orphan population had no access to the truth.

Currently, these mechanisms are failing. The Galactic Ancestral Alliance (G.A.A.) is actively turning off Overlays, revealing gold statues at the North Pole and preparing to completely remove the Projection Dome. To counter this, the parasites prepared Project Bluebeam—an advanced holographic fake alien invasion—intended to trigger mass terror as a final attempt to harvest Loosh before their ultimate removal.

The complete uninstallation of the Three Strings is mandatory for survival. When the impending EMF (Electro Magnetic Frequency) flash occurs, the removal of the projection dome and the terrifying reality of the peeling sky will cause catastrophic psychological collapse, heart attacks, and suicides among those tethered to Religion, Finance, or Perceived Knowledge.
