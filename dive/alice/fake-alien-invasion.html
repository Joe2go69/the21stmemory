Overview

The Fake Alien Invasion is a staged, highly advanced holographic event engineered to occur during the final stages of the Ascension Event (also referred to as The Great Spiritual Awakening). Orchestrated to coincide with global lockdowns and the Emergency Broadcast System (EBS), this event utilizes advanced visual technologies to simulate a planetary extraterrestrial attack and mass abductions. Its primary function is to serve as a calculated "scare event," inducing severe psychological trauma and panic among the unawakened population. This extreme emotional state is a necessary mechanism designed to permanently hardwire the memory of this epochal transition into the soul-architecture of the survivors, ensuring that a parasitic takeover of the physical plain can never occur again. The invasion sequence culminates in the Sky Event and the EMF (Electro Magnetic Frequency) flash, which radically alters the demographic and physical reality of the realm.

Project Bluebeam

Advanced holographic projection technology used to simulate UFOs and extraterrestrial abductions in the sky, creating a visually convincing but entirely fake alien attack.

EMF (Electro Magnetic Frequency) / The Flash

G.A.A (Galactic Ancestral Alliance)

Sleepers

True Taran humans or extraterrestrial souls who failed to awaken prior to the event and will consequently experience the invasion in a state of severe trauma.

Core Revelations

The defining characteristic of the Fake Alien Invasion is that it involves absolutely no physical extraterrestrial threat on the ground. The perceived hostile spacecraft are entirely holographic projections running a software program, devoid of actual occupants or physical substance. While there will be some legitimate, high-density extraterrestrial craft stationed at high altitudes to observe the display, these benevolent beings will not land, engage, or interact with the population below, as the surface environment remains highly dangerous and their intervention in the necessary human trauma is strictly prohibited.

The advanced holographic technologies powering this event are generated by benevolent forces. This specific technology is located in the "Valve" at Mt Meru / Black Rock / Hyperborea, situated at the geographic center of the realm (the North Pole). From this central position, the holograms can be projected outward to cover the entire realm inside the ice wall. The resulting chaos—featuring crowds sprinting in sheer terror and attempting to flee an inescapable holographic sky battle—is not an accidental byproduct, but a meticulously planned energetic and psychological mechanism required for the Ascension sequence.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The mechanics of the Fake Alien Invasion follow a precise sequence of events designed to maximize psychological impact and dismantle the existing 3rd-density paradigm:

  • The EBS and Peak Fear: The event occurs towards the end of the EBS lockdown. The population will already be in a state of profound shock from the EBS broadcasts, which unequivocally expose the systemic atrocities, child sacrifice, and satanic control structures that have governed the planet. The religious and elderly populations will have suffered massive casualties due to the shock of these revelations.

  • Holographic Abductions: The sky will fill with Project Bluebeam UFOs. Television broadcasts and direct observation will show groups of people (hundreds at a time) being levitated off the ground and beamed up into these craft. This will trigger a desperate, directionless flight response among the masses.

  • The Sky Event (Dome Removal): Concurrently, the G.A.A will initiate the removal of the Projection Dome. This dome currently sits inside the Firmament, obscuring true reality. As it is switched off, the sky will appear to undergo "melting pixelation" from the star Polaris downward. This will expose the Dark Matter Field behind it, which is not black "space" as traditionally taught, but a realm of bright white light.

  • The 30-Second EMF Flash: The climax of the invasion is the EMF. A bright white light will envelop the realm for exactly 30 seconds. During this flash, the G.A.A will strip away the remaining reality Overlays.

  • The Aftermath: Once the flash dissipates, the holographic UFOs will vanish. The physical landscape will display pixelation, exposed harmonic scaffolding, and devastation, which the surviving Sleepers will incorrectly attribute to alien energy weapons or nuclear strikes (though nuclear weapons are definitively stated to be a hoax and will not be used). Most critically, 97% of the population will have vanished, leaving only an estimated 520 million people on the planet.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Fake Alien Invasion is the operational climax of a 178,000-year conflict against 4th-density parasites (such as the Custodians, Anuk/Anunnaki, and Grey ETs). These parasitic entities previously controlled Gateway-10, utilizing human vessels as a loosh and adrenochrome harvesting matrix.

The necessity of the scare event is deeply intertwined with the "3 Strings" that hold the unawakened population in bondage: Religion, Finance, and Perceived Knowledge. Because the NPC majority and the unawakened true souls rigidly cling to these false paradigms, a gentle transition to 5th+ density reality is impossible. The sudden, overlapping trauma of the EBS disclosures and the Fake Alien Invasion forces a complete psychological collapse. This collapse shatters the Amnesia Vortex and bypasses the ingrained societal programming, forcing the survivors to abandon their reliance on false science (such as a spherical Earth in dark space), false religious deities, and useless financial systems.

Strategic Implications

Strategically, the Fake Alien Invasion serves as the ultimate fail-safe for Source Creation. By driving the surviving 3% of the population into a state of unparalleled terror and catatonia, the Galactic Ancestral Alliance ensures that the visceral memory of this parasitic infiltration is permanently encoded into the collective soul-architecture of the Taran humans and incarnated Starseeds. This energetic branding guarantees that the species will never again allow themselves to be subjugated, nor will they ever again entertain the hubris of separating from Source or selling their souls.

Following the disappearance of the NPC population and the dissipation of the EMF flash, the surviving true souls will be left in a quiet, heavily altered physical environment devoid of animal or bird noise. From this state of ruin, true memory retrieval will begin. The survivors will gradually access 178,000 years of withheld memories, reuniting with their cosmic soul families, and beginning the process of healing and rebuilding the physical plain through restored abilities of harmonic manifestation.
