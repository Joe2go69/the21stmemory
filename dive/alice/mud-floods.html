The overwhelming physical evidence of these Re-sets survives in plain sight as heavily suppressed historical anomalies, repurposed grand architecture, buried remains of colossal humanoid beings, and the systematic downgrade of both technology and aesthetics deployed to control the newly installed, heavily cloned population.

  • Oopa's (Out Of Place Artefacts) — Blatant physical evidence of the high-technological civilizations that existed prior to recent Re-sets, routinely confiscated and hidden in museum basements by covert authorities to protect the fraudulent timeline of human evolution.
