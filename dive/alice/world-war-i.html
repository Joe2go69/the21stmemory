World War I

Overview

World War 1 (1914–1918) was not a natural geopolitical conflict sparked by the assassination of a political figure, but a highly orchestrated demographic cull designed to permanently sever humanity’s connection to the Old World. Occurring immediately after the culmination of the most recent planetary Reset, the war was engineered to systematically eradicate the surviving generation of young adults and teenagers who still retained suppressed knowledge of reality, herbal medicine, and the true structure of the Flat Earth. By exterminating 15 to 22 million individuals, the controlling entities successfully cleared the demographic landscape to install a newly manufactured, compliant society of cloned Orphans and NPCs (Non-Player Characters), allowing for the total fabrication of modern historical, scientific, and medical paradigms. This global slaughter was a prerequisite for cementing the illusion of linear human progress and hiding the ruins of Great Tartary.

Core Revelations

The conventional historical narrative surrounding the outbreak of World War 1—specifically the assassination of Arch Duke Ferdinand—is entirely fabricated. The true objective was to target and eliminate everyone over the age of 16 who had survived the recent Reset but was not already locked away in an asylum.

In the 1860s, prior to the standardization of state-regulated curriculums, most adults understood that the earth was flat and taught this truth to their children at home. These children grew up to become the exact generation drafted into the trenches of World War 1. By killing these men, the controlling elite eradicated the last vestiges of truth regarding the earth's architecture, ensuring the subsequent generation would accept the Copernican globe deception without resistance.

Furthermore, the war was weaponized to destroy practitioners of natural medicine. Because the incoming control grid required a sickened population dependent on the emerging Big Pharma cartel, the millions of individuals who possessed extensive herbal experience had to be removed from the gene pool. This demographic purge guaranteed that future generations would rely solely on synthetic medications with debilitating side effects.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The mechanics of the WW1 cull must be understood within the direct timeline of the preceding Reset. The last major Reset concluded its destruction in America around 1860 and in the UK around 1900. The aftermath of this destruction left the landscape physically ruined and the population decimated through torture, rape, and mass sacrifice.

To manage the survivors and engineer a new society, the parasites employed a tripartite strategy:

  1. Containment of Adult Witnesses: Any adult who survived the Reset and witnessed the overt reptilian and parasitic slaughter was driven catatonic by the trauma. They were swept off the streets and incarcerated in Lunatic Asylums. These facilities, though constructed for different purposes in the Old World, were repurposed as massive containment zones where the agonizing trauma of the inmates was continuously harvested as Loosh to feed the demonic hosts of sold-soul proxies.

  2. Artificial Repopulation: With the original children and adults consumed during the Reset, new populations were manufactured. Stem cells were taken from children prior to their sacrifice and used to grow clones in subterranean facilities known as D.U.M.B.S (Deep Underground Military Bases). These cloned children were transported via Orphan Trains to fully functioning, empty Tartarian cities to repopulate the realm. To raise them, specific NPC adults had been "saved" in temporary safe zones prior to the Reset and were brought in to indoctrinate the clones with the new, falsified history.

  3. The Global Cull (WW1): The remaining demographic—those in their late teens to mid-50s who possessed Old World knowledge but avoided the asylums—were rounded up and sent into the mechanized slaughter of WW1. Between 1914 and 1918, the Allied Powers and Central Powers were forced into a conflict that yielded 15 to 22 million deaths (9–11 million military, 6–13 million civilian). This mass death event effectively cleansed the realm of anyone capable of disputing the incoming narrative.
