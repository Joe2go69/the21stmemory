Overview

The Orphan Trains are a critical logistical component revealing the true nature of historical timelines and the cyclical destruction of advanced human civilizations. These transport networks were utilized to distribute a newly manufactured population of children across the landscape following a planetary purge, known as a Re-set. Rather than representing a natural historical progression or a philanthropic response to urban poverty, these repopulation efforts expose a meticulously orchestrated system designed to erase all knowledge of the preceding Tartaria civilization, reboot human awareness to a state of absolute ignorance, and provide a continuous supply of energetic and physical sustenance for parasitic entities. The evidence of these events shatters the fabricated narrative of human progress, exposing the systemic manipulation of physical reality and the biological harvesting of humanity.

Key Terminology

Orphan Trains

Logistical rail networks used to transport newly generated, parentless children across continents to repopulate fully functioning but emptied cities following a planetary purge.

Re-set

A cyclical, planned planetary destruction and mass sacrifice event that annihilates the existing population, falsely recorded in historical narratives as the natural "Rise and Fall" of civilizations.

D.U.M.B.S.

Deep Underground Military Bases; subterranean facilities where the new "orphan" crop was artificially grown in laboratories using harvested stem cells.

Oopa&#39;s

Out Of Place Artefacts; blatant physical anomalies and advanced architectural remnants that contradict the fabricated historical timeline and serve as evidence of highly technological prior civilizations.

Tartaria

The advanced, free-energy global civilization that existed prior to the current era, systematically destroyed and hidden during the most recent Re-set.

NPCs

Non-Player Characters; soulless, artificially generated beings that comprise the vast majority of the new populations, designed to uphold the fabricated consensus reality.

Loosh

A negative energetic resonance generated through human suffering, terror, and trauma, utilized as literal sustenance by parasitic entities and demons.

Adrenochrome

A biological substance extracted from children during extreme trauma and sacrifice, representing the primary harvestable product of a Re-set.

Core Revelations

  • The children photographed on Orphan Trains were not the tragic survivors of natural poverty or disease; they were primarily clones, artificially generated without parents in subterranean D.U.M.B.S..

  • During a Re-set, the entire surface population—including all adults and children—is subjected to extreme torture, sexual violence, and mass sacrifice to extract Adrenochrome and Loosh. Before the children were sacrificed, their stem cells were harvested to engineer the next generation of the population.

  • Re-sets do not occur simultaneously across the entire planet. They are executed country by country over several years, utilizing dimensional Overlays and territory shielding to render the active destruction invisible to neighboring regions or seafaring vessels.

  • The last Re-set in America began its repopulation phase around 1728, with the grandchildren of these original orphans eventually driving the fabricated "Gold Rushes" of 1848 and 1851.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Repopulation Logistics

After a Re-set concluded and the free-energy technology was either dismantled or hidden—a suppression period taking 3 to 5 years—the newly grown crop of orphans was transported via train to fully functioning, highly developed cities. These destination cities already possessed intricate infrastructure, harmonic architecture, and free-energy rail networks created by the previous inhabitants.

The Re-education Apparatus

Pre-selected survivors—often Freemasons with prior knowledge of the event—were spared from the Re-set and hidden in temporary accommodations or "Arks". Once the immediate devastation concluded, these surviving adults were deployed to educate and indoctrinate the parentless cloned children. They established a completely fabricated history, including the false biological doctrine of Evolution via natural selection, ensuring the orphans had no connection to actual reality.

Eradication of Legacy Knowledge

To guarantee the orphans never learned the truth of the prior world, targeted extermination events were orchestrated. World War 1 was engineered specifically to cull the remaining population aged 15 to 50. This demographic consisted of individuals who had survived the Re-set and still retained memories of the flat earth, natural herbal remedies, and the old-world civilization. The war successfully eliminated 15 to 22 million people, severing the final links to pre-Re-set knowledge.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The Railroad Expansion and Giant Discoveries

The massive expansion of railroads, which facilitated the deployment of the Orphan Trains, inadvertently sliced through ancient burial mounds left over from previous Re-sets. This excavation uncovered the skeletal remains of over 10,000 Giants, ranging from 12 to 35+ meters in height. The Smithsonian Institution, an entity founded by the same Freemason railroad magnates managing the new infrastructure, immediately confiscated and suppressed these artifacts to protect the false historical timeline.

Mud-floods and Architectural Repurposing

The Orphan Trains operated amidst a landscape recently devastated by Mud-floods—the result of advanced soil liquefaction and energy weaponry used to bury Tartarian cities and turn organic matter into stone. The resplendent buildings that survived this destruction were falsely attributed to the new, primitive populations and repurposed as modern government buildings, libraries, and schools.

Lunatic Asylums as Energetic Batteries

Survivors who witnessed the brutal, demon-led reality of the Re-set and were not slated for the re-education apparatus were incarcerated in massive, repurposed Tartarian structures designated as Lunatic Asylums. Because it takes 20 to 30 years for the new orphan population to mature enough to provide a steady supply of sacrifice and energy, these 5,000-bed asylums served as massive "Loosh Batteries," sustaining the parasitic demons with the concentrated terror and trauma of the shell-shocked inmates during the interim period.

Strategic Implications

  • The implementation of the Orphan Trains was a mechanism of absolute control, ensuring the foundational population of the current era began as a blank slate, entirely dependent on a fabricated, parasitic system.

  • The narrative surrounding the "Industrial Revolution" was a deliberate inversion. Rather than representing an era of human progress and invention, the period of the Orphan Trains marked the abrupt and devastating end of a superior, mathematically and harmonically perfect civilization.

  • Recognizing the Orphan Trains as a mechanical artifact of the Re-set reveals the true purpose behind modern institutions. The global educational, financial, and religious systems were specifically designed by the victors to maintain mass amnesia, suppress multidimensional awareness, and secure complete compliance from the cloned populations until the next planned harvest.
