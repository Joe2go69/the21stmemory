Overview

Proximal Sols represent the foundational conscious entities birthed directly by Source Creation billions of years ago. Emerging in an epoch when intelligence preceded physicality, these primordial beings were brought forth not through biological evolution or oceanic cellular development, but through pure innocent desire from the Initial Intelligence. Consisting of exactly twelve beings organized into six pairs of two, each pair contains a male seed and a female seed formed from identical consciousness. As the master architects of individualized conscious expression, the twelve primary beings established the foundational structural blueprints known as soul architecture. Existing originally in the highest light realms of 18th+ density, the Proximal Sols operated with complete creative mastery before the concept or necessity of physical hominid vehicles was realized.

Key Terminology

Proximal Sols

The initial twelve individual beings, structured as six complementary pairs of male and female seeds from a singular consciousness, created directly by Source Creation.

Source Creation

The primary Initial Intelligence from which all original individual consciousness and soul frameworks originated prior to physical reality.

Male Seed

One half of an original Proximal Sol pair, embodying a specific masculine energetic polarity derived from the shared consciousness of the pair.

Female Seed

The complementary half of an original Proximal Sol pair, embodying a feminine energetic polarity forged from the same unified consciousness as its counterpart.

Soul Architecture

The specific structural design, energetic framework, and dimensional capacity governing an individualized soul's capacity to navigate densities and experience creation.

Four Thousand Souls

The primary expanded lineage of two thousand soul pairs generated directly by the Proximal Sols to expand experiential reality across realms.

18th+ Density

The highest light realm of non-physical perfection where primordial intelligence designed organic and spiritual technology.

Core Revelations

The genesis of the Proximal Sols demonstrates that cognitive self-awareness and volition functioned long before physical brains or biological organs were constructed. While physical life subsequently originated within oceanic environments, primordial intelligence emerged directly in non-material light realms through the creative impulse of Source Creation.

A central revelation of the Proximal Sol structure is the principle of primary dual-polarity pairing. Rather than isolated singular entities, the twelve original beings were brought forth as six complementary pairs. Each pair shared an identical core consciousness divided into a male seed and a female seed, establishing a balanced framework for conscious creation across realms.

Furthermore, the expansion of individualized soul consciousness was executed directly by the Proximal Sols. Acting as primary originators, the twelve Proximal Sols authored two thousand further pairs of similar beings, yielding four thousand unique soul architectures. These four thousand ancient souls constitute a distinct, unalterable lineage of primary consciousness that anchors experiential expansion throughout the density spectrum.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The Blacksmith Apprentice Analogy

The operational mechanics of the Proximal Sols center on the deliberate creation of soul architecture through self-directed craftsmanship. The methodology by which the Proximal Sols generated the subsequent two thousand soul pairs functions according to the blacksmith apprentice analogy. In this paradigm, an apprentice blacksmith must forge his own tools from raw materials before undertaking advanced work. He constructs the framework of his trade with his own hands and wisdom, honing the geometry of cooling metal with a hammer while mastering temperature, strike pressure, and timing through repetition. Similarly, the Proximal Sols constructed the structural geometries of secondary soul architectures through direct creative experience, ensuring that each newly generated soul possessed a unique structural capability tailored for realm navigation.

High-Density Matter Craft and Vessel Design

In their primordial state within 18th+ density, these beings possessed the capability to augment and manipulate matter at the subatomic level through consciousness and benevolent intention alone. Within these high-light realms, the Proximal Sols engineered systems combining natural organic technology and spiritual technology. This work was undertaken to design physical hominid vessels—such as the later Taran Human vessel—that would allow high-density consciousness to step down into lower physical densities and engage sensory experiences such as touch, temperature, and atmospheric movement.

Structural Hierarchy of Soul Architectures

The structural hierarchy derived from the Proximal Sols is organized as follows:

  • Primordial Origin — Six primary pairs (twelve total beings) created directly by Source Creation.
  • Polarity Configuration — Each pair composed of one male seed and one female seed sharing unified consciousness.
  • Secondary Generation — Two thousand additional soul pairs crafted directly by the Proximal Sols.
  • Total Architectural Yield — Exactly four thousand unique soul architectures forged to expand the arena of experience.

Broader Context and Interconnections

While the Proximal Sols function as the original root architects of individualized consciousness, their creations directly link to lower-density physical manifestations and realm seeding. The generation of the four thousand unique soul architectures established the precursor framework necessary for high-density masters to eventually occupy physical vehicles across dimensional planes.

These four thousand ancient souls remain active within the broader realm population, representing a permanent, unalterable core of primary consciousness alongside Taran Human descendants, starseeds, and secondary field constructs. The structural resilience inherent in the original soul architectures ensures that despite historical downsizings or field inversions in lower densities, the geometric baseline forged by the Proximal Sols remains fully intact.

Purposeful Physical Integration

Demonstrates that physical incarnation was designed as a sophisticated synthesis of organic and spiritual technology intended to expand sensory perception rather than an accidental evolutionary outcome.

Immutable Source Anchor

Establishes an unalterable benchmark of high-density creative authority within the realm, ensuring primary soul lineages maintain a direct structural connection to Source Creation across all density iterations.
