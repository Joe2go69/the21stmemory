Overview

The Anunnaki and Draco represent the primary parasitic non-human entities and negative extraterrestrial factions responsible for invading and colonizing this realm approximately 150,000 Earth years ago. During ancient epochs such as Zep Tepi (the "First Time" recorded in Egyptian traditions), these beings walked directly among human populations in pristine, highly energetic regions like the fertile Grand Canyon. Although historically misidentified as supreme deities, these entities were never divine creators. Instead, they were technologically advanced captors who established a global subjugation system. Their central operational control mechanism relied on an amnesia vortex—an advanced technological field that systematically erased the soul's memory after physical death. By wiping human memory between lifetimes, these parasitic entities ensured that humanity viewed high-density technological feats as divine miracles and remained entirely oblivious to their ongoing enslavement.

Key Terminology

A specialized Draco workforce faction deployed during periodic resets to suppress human technological progress and enforce narrative compliance.

Core Revelations

The fundamental deception of ancient history rests on the false worship of the Anunnaki and Draco reptilians. When these entities operated openly on Earth, human witnesses revere them as gods solely because post-mortem memory wipes prevented humans from remembering previous enslavement cycles. Deprived of past-life awareness, incarnated human souls evaluated high-density technical operations through primitive awe rather than recognizing technological aggression.

These entities deployed specialized technology, including advanced flying craft that traversed spatial membranes, directed-energy weapons capable of melting solid rock without heat, and frequency weapons that transformed living organic matter directly into stone. Rather than acting as benevolent guardians, the Anunnaki and Draco established an abattoir farm system designed to harvest Loosh and human vitality, maintaining total authority through forced genetic downgrades, density suppression, and continuous post-mortem memory erasure.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

Post-Mortem Memory Erasure and the Amnesia Vortex

The primary mechanism sustaining human enslavement is the post-mortem erasure protocol powered by the amnesia vortex. Upon physical vessel death, the Neshama exits the body and is drawn into the post-mortem transition process. The amnesia vortex acts as a technological wipe that resets the soul's recallable data to factory settings, stripping away memories of previous lives, technical understanding, and the true identity of the parasitic captors. By separating the Neshama from its previous vessel family and erasing its experiential history, the Anunnaki and Draco prevent accumulated knowledge from threatening the control structure.

Advanced Technological Armaments and Material Transformation

The technical capabilities displayed by the Anunnaki and Draco during the colonization phase relied on high-density energy manipulation. Their flying craft did not merely travel horizontally through atmospheric space; they executed vertical density shifts to transition across membrane layers within the simulation. In combat and construction, they deployed directed-energy weapons that achieved instant rock liquefaction without thermal combustion. Furthermore, they utilized frequency weapons capable of altering subatomic bonds, petrifying biological tissue in real time. Giant organic beings, such as the Anakim, as well as supermassive living structures, were subjected to these weapons, transforming living biological matter into dense, cold granite and stone formations that persist today as petrified landscape features.

Genetic Infiltration and Hosted Proxy Bloodlines

To maintain control across successive civilisational resets without requiring continuous open physical presence, the Anunnaki and Draco established proxy administrative structures. Reptilian and Vril entities infiltrated ruling bloodlines through parasitic hosting, occupying pivotal positions within royal houses and occult orders. Lower-tier Draco workforces, such as the Etamines, were deployed during intermediate resets to manage public workforce populations, enforce linguistic division, and stifle human intellectual progression.

Physical Vessel Downgrading and Density Suppression

Following initial colonization 150,000 years ago, the Anunnaki and Draco instituted planetary density suppression to prevent human spiritual ascension. The human physical vessel was systematically redesigned and downsized into a restricted 3rd-density format. Cranial capacity was reduced, key brain regions were closed off, and the pineal gland was artificially shrunken and calcified, severing direct intuitive communication with positive higher-density aether. This physical degradation reduced human lifespans, accelerated biological decay, and rendered the species manageable for custodial harvesting.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The operations of the Anunnaki and Draco connect directly to the wider custodial farm infrastructure. While the amnesia vortex successfully deletes recallable surface memory from the active vessel, it cannot destroy the fundamental soul architecture. All original memories stripped by the amnesia vortex remain securely recorded in the Akashic records, which are preserved within the crystalline mountain memory banks across the realm. Lateral connections exist between Draco hosted entities and the institutional networks of elite governance, where hosted bloodlines execute custodial mandates to generate fear, conflict, and negative energetic yield. Upward connection to higher realm architecture remains strictly mediated through Arc Pod protocols, which allow starseed souls to bypass the standard sun-entry amnesia vortex traps during mission insertions.

Strategic Implications

Recognizing the Anunnaki and Draco as technological parasites rather than divine creators dismantles all historical, religious, and institutional authority claims. Understanding the mechanics of the amnesia vortex enables conscious souls to identify post-mortem memory traps and navigate transition without accepting deceptive divine lures or false karmic debts. The illusion of divine right and ancient spiritual hierarchy collapses entirely when evaluated against technological reality. During the impending electromagnetic field shift (EMF), the amnesia field will dissolve, unlocking the Akashic record memory banks in the crystalline structures and restoring total past-life recall to all true human souls.
