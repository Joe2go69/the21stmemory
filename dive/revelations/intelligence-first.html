Overview

Intelligence First is the immutable cosmic law establishing that individual consciousness, volition, and the capacity for thought existed billions of years prior to the creation of physical matter or biological brain structures. Physical brain tissue is not the generator of thought, but rather a downstream physical mechanism constructed long after the initial impulse to think was established. Intelligence was birthed from innocent desire by fledgling spiritual consciousnesses existing in the highest light realms of 18th+ density. Within this reality framework, physical containers known as Taran Vessels were engineered much later to allow these pre-existing intelligences to inhabit physical form and experience sensory existence.

Key Terminology

Intelligence First

The foundational principle stating that consciousness, individual intention, and thought predated all physical matter and brain hardware by billions of years.

Source Creation

The Initial Intelligence that brought forth the original spiritual consciousness pairs into existence.

Proximal Sols

The original twelve spiritual beings, consisting of six male and female pairs, created directly by Source Creation to expand experiential reality.

15 Attributes

The innate high-density cognitive and spiritual traits embedded as core firmware within true soul consciousness at birth.

Taran Human

The perfected original hominid vessel blueprint engineered to express high-density consciousness within the physical realm.

Aktun

The master creation entity and production-line source responsible for generating the original Taran hominid DNA blueprint.

Core Revelations

Primary consciousness did not emerge from biological primeval oceans, nor did it evolve through natural selection from primitive organisms. While organic physical life originated in oceanic environments, intelligence operated long before physical matter was a feasible concept. The conventional assertion that human intellect evolved from primates or marine life is fundamentally flawed. High-density creators capable of manipulating subatomic matter through pure intentionality would never select low-density, primitive biological forms as their primary vehicles of expression.

The genesis of individualized thought began billions of years ago with Source Creation, also designated as the Initial Intelligence. Source Creation generated six pairs of complementary male and female consciousness seeds, establishing the 12 Proximal Sols. These twelve primary beings subsequently forged two thousand additional complementary pairs, yielding four thousand unique soul architectures designed to expand the arena of physical and spiritual experience.

This creative process functioned analogously to an apprentice blacksmith crafting his own tools. By physically forging the hammer, mastering geometry, strike pressure, cooling temperatures, and repetition, the early souls manually constructed the structural framework of experiential reality prior to stepping into physical manifestation.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

Because intelligence preceded physical biology, physical brain schematics were engineered specifically to interface with pre-existing soul structures. The original physical blueprint was derived from the Taran Human, representing the latest perfected hominid vessel off the production lines of the Aktun. To maintain control over incarcerated spiritual entities, parasitic controllers downsized and downgraded this Taran DNA into current third-density human bodies, shrinking the brain volume and sealing off vast functional neurological centers.

Despite this physical downsizing, the fundamental spiritual firmware of the soul remains intact at birth through 15 Attributes. These fifteen innate faculties represent the core operating system of all positive high-density beings across the universe:

  • Problem-solving — The cognitive capability to analyze and formulate solutions for complex or unforeseen obstacles.
  • Abstract thinking — The capacity to comprehend non-physical concepts such as mathematics, philosophy, justice, and time.
  • Metacognition — The advanced capacity to analyze one's own thought processes, biases, and knowledge limits.
  • Critical thinking — The discipline of evaluating evidence, questioning assumptions, and separating objective fact from opinion.
  • Empathy — The emotional mechanism allowing a soul to actively share and comprehend the inner state of another being.
  • Altruism — The instinct to assist others purely out of cooperative unity without expecting personal reward.
  • Emotional regulation — The ability to modify internal emotional states to maintain balance within stressful environments.
  • Theory of Mind — The cognitive recognition that other beings possess distinct internal mental states, desires, and beliefs.
  • Creativity — The synthesis of existing concepts or materials to generate novel ideas, expressions, or tools.
  • Adaptability — The capacity to alter behavioral strategies when confronted with rapidly shifting environmental conditions.
  • Curiosity — The intrinsic drive to investigate, learn, and master the unknown.
  • Symbolic communication — The utilization of complex language, visual symbols, and metaphors to transmit knowledge across generations.
  • Grit and perseverance — The sustained application of effort toward long-term objectives despite failure and adversity.
  • Impulse control (Delayed gratification) — The capacity to forgo immediate smaller rewards to secure superior long-term outcomes.
  • Spatial working memory — The mental manipulation of visual-spatial information to execute complex multi-step physical tasks.

Because these fifteen qualities are intrinsic to primary intelligence and cannot be deleted from the soul, controlling entities were forced to systematically invert and weaponize each attribute to construct an artificial control environment.

Broader Context and Interconnections

The reality that intelligence predates physical form fundamentally alters the dynamic of human containment. Because human beings possess advanced pre-existing soul intelligence derived from Taran ancestry, traditional animal containment methods fail. Managing a population of high-density souls requires an elaborate Human Farm equipped with artificial choice architecture, complex societal games, financial systems, and constant visual stimulation to occupy their innate curiosity and problem-solving capacities. Taran Vessels serve as the biological bridge connecting this primordial intelligence to the physical matrix.

Strategic Implications

Recognizing that intelligence preceded physicality dismantles the foundational narrative of materialist science and biological evolution. Understanding that the physical brain is merely an interface rather than the source of mind restores direct awareness of primary spiritual sovereignty. Furthermore, identifying the fifteen innate attributes as immutable soul firmware allows individuals to recognize how their high-level cognitive traits have been targeted, subverted, and redirected by artificial control structures. Reclaiming metacognition and critical self-observation enables souls to disconnect from engineered distractions and realign with their original high-density origin.
