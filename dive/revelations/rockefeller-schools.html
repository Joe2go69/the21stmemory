Overview

Operating as a core sub-branch of counterfeit academic systems, this network spans primary schools, secondary academies, colleges, and universities to install a fake timeline and eradicate historical truth. By replacing the true memory of high civilisations such as Tartaria with fabricated chronologies and pseudo-scientific paradigms, the system functions as a multi-generational cognitive cage that conditions human vessels into lifelong economic servitude and spiritual obliviousness.

Core Revelations

The establishment of the Rockefeller education system served as a decisive strike against human awareness, replacing organic history with a carefully orchestrated illusion. Foremost among its revelations is that no book available in any public or private learning institution contains actual truth. Every textbook, historical archive, and scientific treatise provided by this system has been constructed to anchor consciousness within a narrow, controllable frequency band.

To hide the recent existence of the global Tartarian empire, bloodline managers inserted up to 2,000 years of fake history directly into the human record. This chronological expansion was executed by altering written dates on historic stone edifices and documents—prefixing dates with the letters 'J' or 'I' (representing Immanuel or Jesus) so that year 882 or 762 was misread as 1882 or 1762, thereby stretching time across manufactured centuries.

To substantiate this expanded timeline, a massive logistical operation was executed between 1840 and 1850. During this precise ten-year window, exactly 18,745 rare, pristine manuscripts and books were distributed by the wagonload into European universities, public libraries, and private collections. These texts, manufactured under climate-controlled conditions to match ink and vellum age profiles while displaying zero wear, successfully invented a 1,000-year medieval period to cover up the destruction and reboot of Tartarian society.

A central function of Rockefeller schoolbooks is the systemic misattribution of advanced harmonic architecture. Structures built by African Tartarians to harvest aetheric free energy were re-labeled in schoolbooks as primitive masonry. For example, the Alhambra in Spain is falsely assigned by schoolbooks to named medieval sultans, specifically Muhammad I Ibn al-Ahmar (alleged founder starting in 1238), Yusuf I, and Muhammad V, concealing the reality that these sites were crystalline healing nodes of an advanced global grid.

Furthermore, the public managers of these educational initiatives maintain strict bloodline continuity. Figures prominent in modern technological and educational policy, such as Bill Gates, operate as extensions of the Rockefeller bloodline, ensuring that school curricula continue to align with population management, medical gatekeeping, and technological control.

Detailed Mechanics and Key Elements

The mechanics of the Rockefeller education system rely on weaponizing innate human attributes against the population. Human faculties such as delayed gratification, adaptability, and curiosity are systematically intercepted during early childhood development. Rather than allowing these traits to foster organic problem-solving and spiritual realization, schoolbooks and administrative structures funnel them into artificial constructs such as school grading systems, standardized testing, and repetitive bureaucratic tasks.

The system maintains its illusion through the deception of its own personnel. Teachers, professors, and university researchers do not consciously propagate falsehoods; they are products of the same institutional conditioning. Because publicly available academic record-keeping only extends back 100 to 150 years at most, educators pass down fabricated narratives under the genuine belief that they represent established fact.

Higher education operates as a specialized control trap, creating what can be termed a superior cage. Students are funneled directly from secondary schools into universities, acquiring substantial financial debt to secure academic credentials such as degrees, honors, and doctorates. Upon graduation, the economic landscape forces these highly educated individuals into basic service roles—such as biochemistry post-grads working in retail or coffee shops—while remaining mentally trapped in the defense of the very academic credentials that enslaved them.

When geopolitical objectives require rapid technological or specialized screening, the educational apparatus implements targeted programs. In 1957, the fake event known as the Sputnik shock was orchestrated to generate public panic. This panic was immediately leveraged to pass the National Defense Education Act of 1958, pouring massive funding into schools to establish Gifted and Talented (G.A.T.) programs. These programs served as an asset pipeline to screen high-IQ children for black-budget projects, MK-Ultra psychological conditioning, and Secret Space Program recruitment.

Academic disciplines such as history, anthropology, and archaeology were structured around interpretation rather than empirical truth. By establishing false foundational concepts—such as Johann Friedrich Blumenbach’s 1795 classification of Caucasian human phenotypes—the system veiled the underground repopulation of new human lines from deep underground military bases under the Caucasus mountains, presenting fabricated racial origin theories in schoolbooks to mask periodic human resets.
